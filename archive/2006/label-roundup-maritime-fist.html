Label roundup Part 2 : Maritime Fist

Maritime Fist Glee Club is an indie label that, as the label boss puts it, focus on experimental, and the improvised, and music that speaks to him and holds his attention. Nice! That means they have an eclectic roster, and they try to find beauty in all kinds of music from noise to rock to folk, and that is something that we also like as well here. :) However the best part of his about page, was this :
No matter how you cut it, this is still a very small, very unprofessional attempt at realizing my dream. But I have plans, moderately-sized plans. At least I've come a long way from the days when I used to put all my spare change in a Pringles can hoping that I would save enough to press that first 7".
Props to all the idealist people around the world and here are a few selections that these generous people put online so that we can enjoy.
You can see the catalog and buy directly using paypal for 7 US bux an album!!!
