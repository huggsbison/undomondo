September 6, 2006 · 2006

Pirahnahead

I've first heard of Detroit based producer Maurice Herd aka Pirahnahead with an EP on Moodymann's Mahogani Records and later on a vocal house track with singer Diviniti and his soulful deep house music has always managed to capture my attention. This time he's on Mahogani Music with his debut CD "Solid (A moment) in the mind of Pirahnahead" which includes the 2004 hit Dreams and more tracks in the vein of his boss Moodymann or the UK house collective The Rurals. Warm rhodes / guitar licks on urban downtempo and house beats and silky vocals. This is truly a no filler urban soundtrack album for your luxury home.
