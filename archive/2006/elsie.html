Elsie

"Elsie is a label project, between musicians friends, to represent our differences and experiences, to help and support eachother... friendly, to share materials and time, to create sounds, to organise some events". A small label, not even a proper label I suppose much more a project, it's highly avantgarde and experimental but accessible. Bettamga has silly beats & vocals like Dat Politics. Miwa Momo Hojo makes Japanese lo-fi glitch-pop like 8 doogymoto on Herbert's Soundslike label, but without the drive and it's more experimental. Princess Oiseau is from France and her music is absolutely minimal only a deep bass, a slow beat and her voice & effects, kinda like AGF but even more minimal.
