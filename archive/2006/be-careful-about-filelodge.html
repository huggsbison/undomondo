Be careful about Filelodge.

ATTENTION

Brent Goodman from The Brother Swimming Beneath Me informed me that there's some kind of popup/hijack problem on filelodgeone of our free space providers, the site seems down and the links don't seem to be working for now in mozilla, however in IE (remember that software?) there's 2 visible popups appearing on the screen and then going away. I don't really know what it is or if it's dangerous. I have clicked them 5 times and nothing bad happened (yet). But till that time, it's better if you don't download the songs stored on Filelodge. (just look for the links @ http://www.fileden.com or http://www.old.undomondo.com on your statusbar.) PS: Nooo, it seems when you click filelodge links in mozilla it hijacks your mp3player to some unknown file and the music stops, what's up filelodge why don't you take the site down till you fix this shit.
