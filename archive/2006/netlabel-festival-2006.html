November 14, 2006 · 2006

Netlabel festival 2006

Good news for the netlabelheads! Here's a 2 day netlabel only festival in Rotefabrik, Switzerland. Including minimalist Andrey Kiritchenko, Micromusic from Switzerland, Headphone Science from the US and more, I guess this kind of events would most definitely help netlabels establish themselves as music providers to bigger audiences. I've made a few selections from the roster and I hope to do an extensive netlabel coverage for October/November soon. Also in the news is a Birmingham event you should not miss. It's the record release party of our friend Sid Peacock's new ensemble Squint. I haven't listened to any tracks from it yet but I'm sure it will be unique and playful. So be there and report back! Closing up is an electro/techno set by German artist Neo Filigrante, which includes tracks from Agoria, Alexander Kowalski, The Hacker and his own. Download it for free here.
First you should download the official sampler chosen by the festival management, it features tracks from all participants and features 11 songs. The following songs are not off the sampler. Andrey Kiritchenko - Bursting Suns Andrey Kiritchenko - Scope of my Perception Headphone Science - Giant Squid/Frail Whale Headphone Science - Never Knew Family Freiraum - Land of Confusion
