Goodbye Blogger

Blogger has trashed my template again for a second time this month. I don't know why it's doing it while I'm posting and not tampering with the template, but what it does is, it deletes 70% of the code and voila there's no index page.. I was going to switch to WP in a month or so, and I was thinking of playing with it today, but instead I have been trying to fix the page from a half assed backup I had from the beginning of the month. I think I shouldn't postpone it any longer.. I'll still be posting, but I'll be working on a switch from Blogger to WP right away. BTW WP2.0 is awesome :) So excuse everything design-wise since I can't be bothered to fix it anymore.
