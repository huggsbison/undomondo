May 31, 2006 · 2006

Me on Netlabel Mixes

A few days ago (ok, I checked and it's more like a month ago) I got a mail from the Netaudio list, that said one of my tracks got featured on a mix by Nata and Lost. The mix is called Net Audio Boutique 2 and can be found here. The track is from my one and only release on Broque, and it's possibly done back in 2002. I don't really listen to that kind of deep/german minimal house as I've already mentioned a few times that the genre is completely stale now, but it still feels nice to see your name in neons! Ok, for another mix that had the same old track from 10/2005 here's Sonic Walker's Trick or Treat Mix, and here's my debut EP from May 2005 a year ago, I hope to finish / release some new stuff this summer, and although the music is nowhere near this EP (because it was finished already and featured stuff from 2002/2003) I still like these songs and maybe you will too! And oh, I forgot, I don't remember the exact date now but undomondo is 1 years old now. I remembered it on the last day! Hugs to everyone who keep coming back to the site, more comments and interaction this year please!
