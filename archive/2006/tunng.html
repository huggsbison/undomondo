Tunng

Now I know Tunng has been featured on many other mp3blogs however they deserve every bit of coverage on every media. We've all been obsessed with 'Pool beneath the pond' since our first listen!. I'm particularly struck with the medieval feeling, somewhere along Sopor Aeternus without the gothic feel. Small European towns under snow, ponds and traditional people spring to mind while listening.. Incredible..
