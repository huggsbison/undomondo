May 10, 2006 · 2006

Forgot it!

How can I forget about one band I've thought of reviewing in the first place. This is 66e, who I've met via mp3hugger's blog. They're a post-rock band from Dublin (like God is an Astronaut) what is it with Ireland about postrock I dunno. This is not devoid of vocals like GIAA, it's pretty well done, in fact it's been some time since I've been affected with postrock this much. Ok I admit, it's only been a few months since I felt in love with Microfilm. Here's some stuff to check out
66e - In a deck of 52 66e - One cast one catch 66e - 66e are home 66e - Scrambled Pictures
