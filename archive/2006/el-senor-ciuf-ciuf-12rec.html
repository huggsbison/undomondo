March 25, 2006 · 2006

El Senor Ciuf Ciuf - 12Rec

The lovely folks at 12Rec supplies us yet another lovely music on a recommendation note from our friend Stephane Obadia of Clumsybeats. Oliviero Farnetti and his friends have prepared a surreal landscape with fresh ideas and high fun factor. The raw bootybass induced opener "I feel like a clean blackboard" has a contrasting poppy vocal line and brass, and "I think I saw a dead person walking yesterday" feels like a noisy country song. However I think the minimal funk of the downer "Two more Rows" is the most mature song and feels like early Herbert stuff, with a certain charm of its own. Ciuf Ciuuuuuuf and download all. The original artwork by Joey Weiser deserves a mention as well. You can check out more of his illustrations at Tragic Planet.
