Microfilm - A journey to the 75th

Hi all, please excuse our absence for the last 3 weeks!

Well I'm sure I've proven I'm not so quick when it comes to doing shit. It took me 3 weeks to import the data from blogger and add plugins to wp, along with other things of course.. (note that i'm just a css/php novice as well :/) . I would have made some posts in the meantime, had blogger stopped acting weird and let me post, but after the import blogger stopped version (no idea why)..

Anyways here's v2.0? of undomondo, with a better layout, archives, tags, neater links and instant reblog of other mp3blogs on the sidebar.... I have listened to some great music in the meantime, and now I shall begin with one of them!

Microfilm is an instrumental rock band from France and has been at the top of my playlist constantly for the last few weeks. They've made a powerful album mostly based on tight drum and guitar work. Although there are no vocals, there are audio snippets from movies (I am a sucker for ephemeral audio from radio/cinema) which fit the music very well. The album certainly has a black and white science movie feel to it. Maybe that's because of the cover work, or their website, I'm not quite sure, but listen to Secret Camera and tell me you didn't feel it!

You will notice that this band has no bells and whistles in their sound, but IMHO that's because they don't need it. These guys have cut the crap and delivered an emotional (but not EMO emotional, doh) yet sincere album. I must admit I've listened to some songs on repeat more than once and I think they do deserve to be heard by a greater audience.
