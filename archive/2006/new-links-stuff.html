September 12, 2006 · 2006

Some news and links

Hooray one of the finest blogs on the blogosphere Aurgasm has finally linked to us! You can check out Dani Siciliano (super vocal talent wife of Matthew Herbert.), Fink, Hanne Hukkelberg and more! A few days ago Dichotomy of Epiphany author Sam has written a comment mentioning their joint venture mp3blog Orang Aural, it's now added to the links on the right. Ultra hard to find stuff from avantgarde artists you should check out the previous posts as well. And also I think Sam's feeling a little bit depressed because he doesn't get enough cheers and comments on DoE (I feel it most of the time as well.) There's never so much avant jazz/rock etc. listeners around and there's even less mp3bloggers, so I hope he continues to do his thing! Another recent find is Loadown, which features extreme metal stuff like grindcore and death metal, stuff like Pig Destroyer, Ion Dissonance, Casketgarden and more accessible stuff like Tool and Lamb of God are on the first page. I have increasingly been listening to grindcore again after I found my old Macabre cds, and I was sporting the idea to post some old/new grindcore but I fear a readers riot?! Priceless wikipedia meta list of songs about things, including categories like death, famous people, drugs, money, robots etc.. US Vocal House producer Alton Miller on Myspace with a video of him djing! And last, something not music related but it has to be pointed out, Top 25 Censored Stories of 2007
