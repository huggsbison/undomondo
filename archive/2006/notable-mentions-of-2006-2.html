Notable mentions of 2006 #2

Part 2 of the series.
  1. A Taste Of Ra - A Taste Of Ra [tinymixtapes]
    2. Bizarre little album on HäpnaBut I agree with the Tinymixtapes review, it's got so few memorable moments, dunno why.
  2. Asobi Seksu - Citrus [pitchfork]
    3. Methinks it's one of the best indie albums this year..
  3. Avishai Cohen - Continuo [past review]
    4. Such a beautiful one, this closer track
  4. Black Ox Orkestar - Nisht Azoy [past review]
  5. Broadcast -The Future Crayon
    6. You know what's wrong about me? I tend to stay away from popular & hyped stuff. Although this instinct has successfully guided me in the music industry since teenage years, every year there's one or two albums that I can't listen because of this subconscious urge. Luckily this time I resisted that urge and listened to the Broadcast album. It would have been in Top 10, had I begun listening earlier.
  6. Burial - Burial [past review]
    7. Congrats to BurialBoomkat's Top 100 chart and also Kode9Hyperdub crew for giving us 2 groundbreakers in a year.
  7. Clark - Body Riddle [pitchfork]
    8. Chris ClarkClarkWarp releases this year.
  8. Diesler - Diggin' it Something Rotten [tiny mix tapes]
    9. Here's Diesler'sDecember Podcast for you to enjoy.
  9. Ekkehard Ehlers - A life without fear [past review]
  10. Esbjoern Svensson Trio - Tuesday Wonderland [past review]
  11. El Perro Del Mar - El Perro Del Mar [pitchfork]
    12. Pleasant folk songs for easy listening
  12. Fred Frith / Stevie Wishart / Carla Kihlstedt - The Compass Log and Lead
    13. Virtually non-existant in all top lists of 2006, but why? This is the most accessible FrithIntakt.
  13. In the Country - Losing Stones, Collecting Bones [NY Times]
    14. Out on one of my favourite labels from the North, Rune Grammofon, that gave us Alog, BiosphereJazzkammer
