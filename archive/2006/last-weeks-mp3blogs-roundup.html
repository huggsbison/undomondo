Last week's mp3blogs roundup

Did you notice there's lots and lots of more mp3blogs every week? It now takes 3+ pages for one day on Hype Machine. Here's a messy links post for the last week, and some nice music I chose among all those! gang gang dance , in tits we trust and a complete bargara morgenstern coverage on Buscate un Novio Chihei Hatakeyama (Kranky) - Bonfire on the field thanks to Camera as Pen (2 more songs on the actual post) Kings of convenience - I don't know what I can save you from (royksopp mix) and 3 more tracks @ disco-not-disco Tons of interesting stuff on Robosexual. Fennesz, Tulsa Drone, Fog, Espers, Fourtet etc. Boards of Canada, Roman Flugel and Dell and more @ A robot cometh John Zorn stuff covering many different phases of his career @ Funtime, OK Frank Zappa - Remington Electric Razor complete bootleg album @ Cable and Tweed AGF & Sue C, Konono no.1, Kettel and more, good choice of artists @ Bricolage Fantasy Camera Obscura @ Who needs radio?. Female vocals and indie pop. More Mclusky @ Big Static! and also Minor Threat, Islands and El-P.. Kemialliset Ystävät, Charalambides, Spires that in the sunset rise and more avant-noise-no wave bands on Warped Reality Magazine. ... and a very funny project done by Ni9e that takes out the non-cussword parts of NWA and gives you the real deal! essential. New Links added : Big Static, Robosexual, Warped Reality Mag, give them love!
