Label roundup Part 3 : If Society

Ending our 3 part label review is the Finnish indie / punk label If Society.
We donÂ´t know all the fine words for what the world or the scene should be like. We just love music and try to spread some open-mindness by putting out and distributing all kinds of stuff from the DIY-underground, be it punk, indie pop, experimental noise, electro or whatever, you name it. Our bands tell the tales of broken things like hearts that could be yours.. So get in touch for friendship and latest releases. We want to come and play in your livingroom.
Yeah and with an extensive media page on their site, they are actually playing in my living room, and they will play in yours too. There are 10 videos and 25 audiosongs on the media site, covering a broad range on the indie/punk spectrum, from the metallic hardcore of Hero Dishonest, to Boys of Scandinavia's electro-indie hybrid and Echo is your Love's solid guitar based indie, good stuff.
You can buy If Society's own releases and other stuff on the If Society shop. Reasonable prices!
