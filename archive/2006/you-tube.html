March 8, 2006 · 2006

You Tube

From now on we'll be trying to reach the bottom of the endless pit that is You Tube. So many great live & otherwise footage hit youtube everyday. It's our duty to bring them to your attention!. The videos will be diverse as usual, but related to music at last! Here's a bunch :
* Jahcoozi - Live and having fun with Ice Ice Baby. Go Sasha baby! * Slayer - Antichrist (Live @ Studio 54) Combat Tour with Exodus, Slayer & Venom in 1985. Good Quality! It's amazing to see Slayer with this much raw energy. Look at Tom Araya's singing, he's screaming like a mutha!. * Mr BungleAir Conditioned Nightmare Live from Bizarre Festival. For all the people who have been living under a rock for so long, Mr. Bungle is Mike Patton's (ex-Faith no More) nuttycrew. This album was shocking at the time for everyone, but now everyone knows how mad he really is. Watch his other band Fantomasdoing Cape Fear live. * The Specials - A Message To You, Rudy Live (Oct 1979) : oldschool ska! * Ryuichi SakamotoTibetan Dance(Live @ Japan 2005) with Mr. Fennesz on guitars. * and something non musical The Simpsons - Intro shot in real life. Priceless!.
