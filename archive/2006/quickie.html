Quickie..

No time for a real post today, so instead of us read these great publications and blogs. :) See you tomorrow!
    Orgy in Rhythm : wow, essential collection of retro jazz here. Stuff that's been left on shelves for long, now exposed. People like Sabu Martinez, Art Blakey, Vannier, Sun Ra etc.. Here's what the owner says "THIS IS DONE FOR NO OTHER REASON THAN TO PROMOTE GREAT MUSIC IN A WORLD OF MEDIOCRE RUBBISH" Tru dat! Awesome Tapes from Africa : Just what the title says! Space City Rock : Music and reviews, like The Essex Green, Mudhoney, Telepopmusik etc. Drink Moloko NY : NY event lists, Links, Photography Splotch Magazine : Submission based online art/photography mag. Great photography features on the last issue. Digicult : Italian portal for netart / videoart / electronic music. Here's their latest issue of Digimag and the previous issue in English.
