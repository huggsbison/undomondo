January 11, 2006 · 2006

Tosca - Souvenirs

Which artists would you like to get remixed by if you had the chance? The amount of music (and the signal to noise ratio) is growing exponentially in todays world, and it's getting more and more important who you ask for remixes today! Fortunately, it's not a problem for Tosca boys. First they are the coolest guys from Vienna. I mean, who would refuse Mr. Dorfmeister or Mr. Huber? Second, they have an exquisite taste in music and this CD is the final proof for me!. These gentlemen asked Bernd Friedman, Senor Coconut, DSL, Rodney Hunter, Stereotyp, AGF, Plantlife, Henrik Schwarz, Lindstrom & Prins Thomas and more.. and guess what it's a great CD. I knew it would be a great CD when I saw the artists, but still I was amazed at the quality of music after the first listen. All of the tracks on this CD has their own character, no fillers, no mediocre jobs and once more another big wow at the choice of artists!
