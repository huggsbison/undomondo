April 20, 2006 · 2006

Vialka videos

Ok, as promised earlier here are some shitty quality videos I've captured from the Vialka show. Sorry it took a bit of time to upload, I've shot the footage with a sony digital camera, so it's not so good, the stage was dark, and the guitarist is not seen most of the time.. Ok so I mean it's highly unprofessional, but it's still a rare footage, and I'm pretty confident in that you'll be amazed to watch this duo live :) So without further ado : the first clip begins with some of Shitty Monkeys, but then (I've forgotten to cut it correctly) jumps to Umfall, one of my favourite Vialka songs, this is the longest clip, and it has Marylise playing drums and singing!. On the second clip Marylise sings the beginning of Only the wrong Survive, another favourite.. and Marylise dancing to a funny song.. There are 2 more short clips you can find here and here. There were much better parts of the show that I couldn't capture, like Marylise's jump into the crowd and Eric's dance and costume etc. but this I hope is enough to convince you to go their shows, if they ever get to your city (and I'm sure they will.)
