December 28, 2006 · 2006

Notable Mentions of 2006 #1

Well, I'm still in a hurry and can't devote enough time to undomondo, but I don't want to finish 2006 without mentioning all who deserve to be mentioned. I've got around 30 albums/bands to mention here in my list, but uploading one song for all takes some time. Here are some notable mentions in random brain order mode to be continued and concluded hopefully tomorrow.
  1. Aceyalone And RJD2 - Magnificent City [Prefix Mag]
    2. Not everything is great on this one but the first part's not to be missed.
  2. Andrea Belfi - Footprints [past review]
  3. Belbury Poly - Belbury Poly [past review]
  4. Call me poupee - Western Shanghai [past review]
  5. First Nation - First Nation [past review]
    6. Female Trance, may be the best tune of this year.
  6. Hasan Yarimdunia - Dardanelles Turquie Gelibolu
  7. I'm not a gun - We think as Instruments [past review]
  8. Leels - Pleased [past review]
    9. So underrated, I think the last time I checked they've got a few thousand or so listens at last.fm. It's a shame, it's such a beautiful album.
  9. Paavoharju - Uskallan EP
    10. First on Miasmah, now on Fonal, Paavoharju makes really special music.
  10. Root 70 - Heaps Dub [past review]
  11. The Cracow Klezmer Band - Zuriel [past review]
  12. Zakarya - 413 A
    13. Another Tzadik album, this time from Paris based Zakarya. Also a strong contender for the top 10, I don't know how I haven't mentioned them before.
