November 22, 2006 · 2006

Reblog 11.22

* CSS "Alala" (princess superstar dj's are not rockstars remix), Architecture In Helsinki "It's 5" (Poutine Dream Mix 33hz & Ming) and most importantly Fanfare Ciocarlia's Romanized version of "Born to be wild" on Pardon my Freedom. Also Benga and Skream if you like some dubstep. Added to our permanent linklist. Hi guys! * Astrud Gilberto's Ziggy Ziggy Za on Bushwick is Beatiful, Astrud is truly magical. You can also get Koop - Waltz for Koop but it's YSI so you've gotta hurry, it's a classic downtempo/nujazz song, if you didn't know. * New Compilation on Communications Major, English Minor, featuring downloads from bands such as The Weird Weeds, Low, Magyar Posse, Rocky Votolato and if you don't know any of these names there's a Leonard Cohen song thrown in the mix as well! ;) * Revep - a new reviews only blog by our friend Winjer. Mostly reviewing and rating minimal/electro house and techno 12"s. Check out his Ricardo Villalobos review. It's in Turkish but the ratings are universal, so that's no excuse for not checking out. * New project/supergroup from Damon Albarn (Blur and Gorillaz), Paul Simonon (The Clash), Simon Tong (The Verve) and Tony Allen (Africa 70 and Fela Kuti) called The Good, The Bad and The Queen! on Hate Something Beautiful, also check out 2 covers from Of Montreal, Fujiya & Miyagi and MGMT.
