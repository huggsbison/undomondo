Netlabels - Other

Here's the second part of the netlabel report. I fixed a small issue with comments so it's commentable once again, and I'm hoping the recent lack of comments were because of that :). Minimal Techno & Dub
Caleb Fox – Amnxio . Nika Japaridze – Traginew Nika Japaridze - Fireworks Emmerichk – Frandalanke (Bifidus Broken Heart Dub) Emmerichk – Morontia Pablo Akaros – Codigo de Barras
Very deep kick and lush pads on Caleb Fox's debut on Mmmph Music and 2 shuffle tracks from Nika Japaridze on Rest. This is some creative stuff, there's a lovely bass on Fireworks and this boy from Tbilisi, Georgia surely knows how to write melodies that can separate his tunes from the rest of the pack. More tunes on myspace. From Georgia we go to Mexico and South America, Filtro netlabel from Mexico, which occasionally features stuff from Fax constantly releases good minimal house/dub with rhythmic blips. Good deep stuff from Emmerichk and a lovely remix by Bifidus who also has a track on Filtro15 compilation Bitflow. Other
