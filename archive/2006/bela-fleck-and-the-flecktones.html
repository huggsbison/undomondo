Bela Fleck and the Flecktones

I've known Mr. Fleck's name for a few years, mostly because of his "Ten from little worlds album", and if I weren't lazy (and ill) I would post some songs from that album as well.. All the time I thought he was the bass player, or the composer or the guitar player, but I never thought he was the banjo player. Yes, banjo.. and quite good at that too.. He's even won a grammy!. The music of Bela Fleck and the Flecktones, is hard to label, he's using banjo but not making country music, the songs are a melodic fusion of bluegrass, jazz, world music, funk and whatever comes their way.. eclectic to say the least. The Flecktones are a great band as well, they are not a `support` band to Bela Fleck, but they are great musicians themselves. Their new one "The Hidden Land" is clearly the best album I've listened to in February, once again it has more than enough songs to choose from. The first song is lead by a soothing flute melody, with great basswork in the middle. The second song is lead by Bela's banjo, and has a 90s pop-jazz approach to it, be sure not to miss this album, as it has great instrumentation and many more cool melodies/songs. This is avant bluegrass (ok I've made that up.) at its best, and should I add .. no vocals!
