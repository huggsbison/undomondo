February 24, 2006 · 2006

RSS feed change.. important..

Folks, last night I was wondering about the decrease trend after checking out the user logs, and I figured out that i've got around 6000 404 errors on (guess what) the ex-RSS feed at /rss/atom.xml, there were many subscribers to the old rss feed on the web, and many more on desktop aggregators as well.. I checked the old feed, and it seemed dead, and didn't show any post after the switch to wordpress. I panicked. So what I did the first thing in the morning was to redirect the old feed to the feedburner feed, but I had to work a few hours to get it correct I guess, I only want the feedburner feed to exist, but I had to get this message to all the subscribers at the old feed address.. I tried to take measures to do it, but I don't know if I'm successful or not, we'll see. Apart from that, I've also added del.icio.us links to the feed and changed its template.. Feedburner has invaluable new options, that take your feed to a new level.. So now our feed has gotten from lousy to mediocre, thanks to feedburner! :) So to sum it up everyone this is the one and only feed you should subscribe to : feeds.feedburner.com/undomondo Now there's also an email subscription link powered by feedblitz. If you are being stubborn about not using RSS, you can subscribe using your email, and feedblitz will send you an update whenever there's something new (and that's probably no more than 3 times a week.) And also don't forget to check out the recent links on the sidebar for a mini reblog of posts from other mp3blogs that I like. You can go and download them all too..
