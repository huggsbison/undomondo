Stuff worth mentioning

image from Stencil Revolution. * Melt Banana - We will rock you (Queen cover) on Cacophony and Coffee. * Incredible cover of Dead Kennedys' Too Drunk to Fuck by Nouvelle Vague Fabulist!. * Gnarls Barkley- The Boogie Monster on I heart music and 8 Gnarls Barkley - Crazy versions on Brigadoon * Barbara Morgenstern - Alles was lebt bewegt sich and Husky Rescue - New Light of Tomorrow on Bricolage Fantasy. * Hot Chip – Boy from School (Erol Alkan Extended Re-work) on Who needs Radio?. * Savath and Savalas on You can call me Betty (YSI). * 2 tracks from Farben, an influential side project of Jan Jelinek, which has been one of my alltime favourite clicky, deep, minimal house projects, featured on Robosexual. * Hote - Binary EP on Argentinian Vegroo netlabel. Downtempo & Electronica. adcBicycle on Kikapu netlabel via disco selector. * Influential minimal techno artist Monolake Live at JJJ Melbourne on the ill-ec-tro-nic. * Classic oldschool house track by Farley Jackmaster Funk & Jesse Saunders (YSI) on Chantilly Bass. * Niobe - 2 tracks from her White Hats CD. Resembling Dani Siciliano and H. Hukkelberg. via Getecho. * Sleepless mix featuring ambient & jazz on Motel de Moka, more Jeff Gauthier Goatette please!. * The Fiery Furnaces and El Perro del Mar on Six Eyes. * Huge number of rare/bootleg Radiohead, The Pipettes, The Rakes stuff and a live version of Final Fantasy's - If I were a carp on The Torture Garden. * Tracks from Beirut & Asobi Seksu on Funtime OK. * More Asobi Seksu on Silence is a rhythm too and My Old Kentucky * Camera Obscura - Lloyd, I'm ready to be Heartbroken on Rewritable Content. * and a slew of Medeski, Martin and Wood on Feed me Good Tunes.
