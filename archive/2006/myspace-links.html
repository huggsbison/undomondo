September 23, 2006 · 2006

Myspace links September #1

Ok, here's something I've been trying to finish for some time, a new myspace band linkdigs.. Most are bedroom musicians and unsigned acts, 1. Brontosaurus Records. Nu italodisco and house music by this small Frankfurt label. Lead by my dear friend Phillip Lauer who was previously one half of Separe Recordings, I think they will turn out to be a pretty successful label soon, check out if you like Metro Area / Daniel Wang stuff mixed with soulful German dance music. 2. Arto Mwambe. Hot Prospect Arto Mwambe from Africa! Check out his Hum Along track! and buy his Punkt release on Boomkat.
When Arto arrived from Africa in order to make some fast & dirty music, equipped with fine & basic production gear Arto Mwambé came up with a quite unique ep including 2 tunes to tear the roof off and the sucker, and possibly make him famous in the western hemisphere.
3. Lowfrequency. Experimental glitch/noise.
Lowfrequency - Fracture
4. Voconut. A Turkish electronica collective focusing on IDM and experimental electronica.
obookubo - missileman koko uno – turist
5. 2/5bz. One of the most original Turkish electronic artists ever (now confirmed to be living in Turkey.), 2/5bz's music can be defined as Orientalcore ,Belly Dance Punk ,Electro Improvise Folk ,Middle Eastern Visualsound System ,Turkish Dub ,Militant Oriental etc, kitsch cut-up music full of Turkish samples and cultural humor.
2/5bz - Karabesk 2/5bz - Kurtulus Yok 1994 2/5bz - Live at Transmediale
6. Dj Ahmedinejad aka Hugo Chavez. Noisy Middle eastern electronic mash similar to 2/5 bz – porest and Muslimgauze 7. The Collapsing Lung
"The Collapsing Lung duo play an inventive set of noises made from junk stirred in a battered suitcase, a cowbell and scissors on a guitar, adding to it a dither of computer digitalia that references Sun City Girls and Nobukazu Takemura alike." Lee Henderson The Wire
8. Man called Adam. Cut/paste samples, experimental electronic music with guitars & downtempo a bit amateurish on the mixdown and needs better mastering but has got some interesting bits.
Man called Adam - Where are the flying cars also checkout This is hi-fi from his page.
9. Thruoutin Electronica and minimal glitch.
Thruoutin - Find a way to do it
10/ Red Audix. Dancefloor oriented minimal techno from Germany.
Red Audix - Keyboards Red Audix - Istanbulism
