Christmas Reblog

Merry xmas to all who celebrate Christmas today!!. * Funtime OK's 2006 list. incl. Burial, Joanna Newsom, Battles, and lists Destroyer & The Knife on disappointments. * A very informative Joanna Newsom live review and 2 songs on Under the Rotunda. * Bubbachops' New Year List for 2006 on Motel de Moka. Varied list, haven't heard most of these at all. (apart from Charalambides and Hisato Higuchi). Make sure you listen to the incredible stoner-psych rock from Om. Thanks! * Classic Christmas songs, performed by artists like Bing Crosby, Frank Sinatra, Dean Martin & more on Instrumental Analysis. * Communications Major's top 10 reissues of 2006. incl. Sebadoh's III, Sibylle Baier, Os Mutantes. * Top Five on White Boy Dance Floor, including Hot Chip, Herbert and Spank Rock. * The Indie Girl of 2006 vote. * Stones Throw Xmas selection by Peanut Butter Wolf. * Radio set from dubsteppa Skream on Barefiles. * The Replacements on Buddyhead. * 3 Peel Sessions from Kwaya Na Kisser. Wire from 2002, The Hives and Liars.
