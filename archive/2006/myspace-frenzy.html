November 15, 2006 · 2006

Myspace frenzy!

Different genres and quality, this month's worthy finds on myspace. * Oturma Grubu : Minimal electronic pop with postrock influences from Ankara/Turkey. * 1 Abaco Autor Ricar : Well, even the name of this guy is strange. An interesting hybrid music, 90's rave meets new age meets Spanish romanticism and guitar solos? You name it! * Seige : Dope soulful hiphop beats. Check out Oan One and Just to feel good. * Crescent Heights : Gentle guitars and vocals evoking images of pixies in an enchanted forest. from Vancouver.
Go down South Disappearing Forest
* Marconi Union : Again, gentle electronic soundscapes with layers of subtle changes, suitable for rainy driving sessions. Check out Sleepless. * Mark Hardbone : DJ and Producer Mark Hardbone from Berlin. Good lead on this one.
Popcorn v5
* Middle Distance Runner : Washington DC is our city and we like it that way. We give out CDs for free, sometimes with unique drawings. Middle Distance Runner plans to spiral out of control and implode by 2015. In fact, make that a promise. More info on Instrumental Analysis
Up in a tree Naturally
* Missy Nelson. Bruce Haack-ish educational samples mixed with lo-fi electronics. Just what I needed!. * Mohanski, Radio friendly indie/twee popish stuff from London.
