July 16, 2006 · 2006

Music information helps grow ears!

  • Just as I was worrying that I may be wrong by not including Liars in my top 2006 lists, Ear farm comes to the rescue from a full bootleg @ Cabaret, Montreal, Canada - 22 June 2006, including a cover of Nirvana's "Territorial Pissings" as encore. Another great find is an 8-bit electronic version of Metallica's Enter Sandman by 386 DX. The vocals in the prayer part is terrific!. I think they can replace Hetfield on the next Metallica with 386DX vocals, that would rock!.
  • Another bootleg EP comes from B(oot)log the definitive bootleg mp3blog. This time it's Regina Spektor at The Current, MPR, 2 May 2006.
  • Concert photographs from the 18th Dour Festival. Including Peeping Tom and Erlend Oye via one of the photographers Gonzale's Deviantart page.
  • FLIKA - MAKUNOUCHI BENTO, BLEEP, SELFMADEMUSIC EP on TMBASE.RO via ektopia. Also free download: Lament of the fishing robot EP by Makunouchi Bento on Camomille
  • Listen to the Boston Typewriter Orchestra via Glimmer.
  • We all know indie/rock/hiphop gets enough coverage so every band gets a chance sometimes. What about music that never gets coverage, but in fact it needs to? That's what our friend Moka did. Top 12 Contemporary music albums 2005-2006, an essential roundup if you are into some great music. Thanks so much.
  • 3 livesets from Canadian minimal/electronic dub artist Deadbeat on Foeweel. YSI link though so hurry.!
