Splendiferous

Splendiferous is Transient's latest on his own Noisy Vagabond netlabel. Ok, so i looked up the dictionary for splendiferous, and it means extraordinarily or showily impressive according to Webster. Fine title, as this is an impressive album (terrible album cover this time though, so I've put up a cover from past releases). Transient is a well-known figure in the netlabel scene that has previously released stuff on all the great netlabels like Monotonik , Kikapu , Camomille and Miasmah and more. Actually I really wonder how can produce so much music. Anyways a diverse album here ranging from drill'n'bass to strange 90's demoscene-esque synthflute solos to breakbeat.
