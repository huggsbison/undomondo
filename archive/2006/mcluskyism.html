April 21, 2006 · 2006

Mcluskyism

As opposed to Kadirizm, Mcluskyism is actually a good thing. I think I first read about them in a review on Buddyhead, after they had disbanded :(, since then I have been feeling sorry about it. Their 2006 album released after Mclusky is no more, is supposed to be a best of album of this nearly-cult band. Pitchfork's Matt Le May called them "asshole indie rock"-- direct, obnoxious, and unapologetic, and they already have a t-shirt called "Mclusky, shit rock". This Welsh trio has a bit of Shellac and Fugazi in it, and I don't know why but a little bit of Suicidal Tendencies and perhaps Nirvana? (or is that Steve Albini?). I really liked their hostile attitude and their goofy lyrics too, it's fun and sincere and not pretentious and crap like most of the trendy indie rock we get these days.
