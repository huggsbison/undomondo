Cassetteboy - Dead Horse & more

The funniest guys in UK are back! Don't you just love the politically incorrect UK guerilla humour? Don't you love the accent?? I sure do, and I've laughed countless times listening to this tape Dead Horse, and their previous bootleg with DJ Rubbish - Inside a whale's cock!. Taking on everything from politics to pop culture, these lads cut and paste audio snippets to make incredibly humorous audio collages. The samples ? well of course they aren't cleared, but who cares! copyright sux! Today's selection includes a J-Lo parody (mariachi with an English accent!), a Streets parody (The Streets should be shot!!), a Harry Potter parody (Harry Potter and the black leather cunt!), and against the usual suspect (George W. Bush is impeached with an occasional Just a Perfect Day sample)! Love ya guys, you're the best!
pitchfork review of their previous album The Parker Tapes.
