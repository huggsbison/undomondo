March 7, 2006 · 2006

Erstes Wiener Heimorgelorchester

After a short trip to Germany in the weekend, we've caught the German flu!. Here's a band I've been thinking of posting for some time, but now under the German influence called the Frankfurter Wuerstl it's imminent. Erstes Wiener Heimorgelorchester (EWHO) is a wacky 4 piece synth electropop band from Austria. I don't know why the synthpop scene is full of goths and serious musicians, but the world needs fun people like our bros at 50 Hertz or Mr Ed Dmx. It's not only S&M , industrial noise and latex you know! These guys have been writing popsongs in German since 1994, and contrary to popular view, made German language sound good with music (I like it anyways!). Their email address has the monochrom.at domain, so I guess they're a prank electro pop band from the legendary Monochrom crew?. For example the first song `penisverlaengerung` is about penisenlengthening and it's actually pretty funny if you know German. 2 Songs from their latest album autoplay are here with some more excerpts from the band on their own site.
