June 20, 2006 · 2006

Sebadoh - III

Hey, look what we have here.. I think I've postponed this a little bit too much, because it's been nearly a month since this remastered release of one of lo-fi indie rock's defining bands hit the stores, and this is one of the most important albums of the 90s lo-fi heritage so it definitely needs the respect!. Sebadoh is the brainchild of Lou Barlow also of Dinosaur Jr. fame, and oh what am I talking about, everybody knows about Sebadoh, so let me shut up here and lead you to this Pitchfork review, where surprisingly Pitchfork gives a suitable score to an incredible album that shaped the sound of the 90s.. 9.3 I chose these 2 songs as a preview, and I really think everyone trying to write an indie love song should listen to "Wonderful Wonderful" to see how they compare to one of the strongest examples, and I have to mention that this remastered Domino release contains 2CDs and 39 more songs, and whole lotta different bundles so do yourself a treat and buy it here unless you own the original har har.. . Here's two more songs, one from the Sebadoh vs Helmet album ", which doesn't feature Helmet contrary to what the title said, and one of my most favourite Helmet songs "Taken".
Listen to more Sebadoh at Domino's audiopage Sebadoh Classic or Dud thread on ILX Helmet Classic or Dud thread on ILX Helmet's Page Hamilton rants about today's punks.
"I hear so many bands that, to me, I can't distinguish between them, he said, talking about the current scene. "It's sort of like, 'Insert four guys with tattoos here.' And it's pseudo-hardcore or pseudo-punk rock or emo. It just seems like you get your 'Rock Star 101' manual and fill in the blanks. This music just doesn't interest me at this point in my life."
