January 3, 2008 · 2008

Part 4 : Top 25 - 15

Alright fellas, I couldn't finish the list on time, but I'll finish what I started hopefully. I'm still in London so I'm trying to wrap this thing up on a borrowed computer. Ahleuchatistas - Even in the Midst… (Cuneiform) : One of the three best math/maze rock albums this year from Asheville, NC's Ahleuchatistas. Past review on undomondo. Thilges - La Double Absence (Staubgold) : Decent electronic/acoustic eastern/western fusion album unlike others. Past review on undomondo. Kerkko Koskinen - Agatha (Ricky Tick) : Cinematic big band jazz stuff by Finnish composer Kerkko Koskinen. Past review on undomondo. DuOud & Abdulatif Yagoub - Sakat (Label Bleu) : Lovely hybrid electronic/acoustic album with roots arabic oud music fused with dubby electronics. Past review on undomondo. Grails - Burning off Impurities (Temporary Residence) : Dark Psychedelic rock from SF. Past review on undomondo. Artanker Convoy - Cozy Endings (Social Registry) : Balanced blend of psychedelic, noise, funk, jazz , rock. Past review on undomondo. Death Ambient - Drunken Forest (Tzadik) : Strings and Noise by the infamous Tzadik improvisers Fred Frith, Ikue Mori and Kato Hideki. Past review on undomondo. Serafina Steer - Cheap Demo Bad Science (Static Caravan) : British leftfield pop by harpist & singer-songwriter Serafina Steer. Past review on undomondo. Rahsaan Patterson - Wines and Spirits (Artistry Music) : Soulful urban electro-funk by Bronx NYC's Rahsaan Patterson. Another review. Lukid - Onandon (Werk) : Delicate stoner loops and sampler electronica by London whizkidLukid. Past review on undomondo.
