June 25, 2008 · 2008

Cenk Akyol - Summer Jazz Mix

Our friend Cenk Akyol mixes another quality mix; before coming back to Istanbul for a vacation after 2 years of living down under!.
It's been a while I haven't post any jazz mixes. Here is a new one, with the excitement of going back home to Turkey for summer holiday, after two years. So the vibe is warm and positive. Classic, instrumental, vocal and contemporary jazz; all blended equally. Pleasure is guarantee to a wide audience! Sit back and enjoy this last selection from Australia, while I'll be enjoying it at Mediterranea in the coming weeks.

Some other mixes : * Another mix that Cenk digged on Awesome Tapes from Africa, is this some slow West African psychedelia and hypnotic guitars mix with no well-known names, which basically spells more treasure hunting. zshare. * Sankofa mix by Zip; courtesy of Lovefingers, Perlon boss and our friend Zip, mixes a terrific slow disco set. * Got tipped by another friend from down under Tim Shiel on a spaced out mix produced by Tom Smith of Cleptoclectics, blending songs by artists like Tarentel, Alog, The Art Ensemble of Chicago, Lukid as well as their own. Get it here * Beat Electric Guest Mix on Cosmic Disco (Specialising in Boogie, Rare Disco, Laser Soul and Proto House, wow!). Here's the Beat Electric blog. 2 more great mixes on Cosmic Disco once again, first one by Stu and the other by Niles which includes rare gems from Spain, Peru, Italy and Australia. * Soul Nation soul mix on 100 Limousines, downright incredible selection by collector/dealer Dave Withers. Don't miss!
