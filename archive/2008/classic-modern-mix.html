January 6, 2008 · 2008

Classic & Modern Mix

Hello all, I'm back in Istanbul, gonna finish the best of list tomorrow and storm into some great new stuff I've been amassing since a few weeks. For today, here's a mix by our friend Cenk Akyol which accompanied me well in a gloomy London morning. Cenk : This selection is made out of my favorite classical and modern pieces (digital processing and a few musique concrete). I blended old and new equally. I assume these warm electronics of modern music won't bother classical listeners. Same for the beautiful classical pieces! Have a pleasant chill... Scanner - Libera Jean SIBELIUS - Le Cygne De Tuonela Goldmund - 25 Thousand Miles Leos JANACEK - On An Owergrown Path_The Madoona Of Frydek (Schiff) Maurice RAVEL - Pavane Pour Une Infante Defunte El Fog - Olive Motor - Mystic Aphex Twin - Gwely Mernans Akira Rabelais - Wyclif Gen. ii. 7 Henry PURCELL - Dido And Aeneas (when i am laid on earth) Erik SATIE - Gymnopedie No. 3 (orchestra by DEBUSSY) Alva Noto - Haliod Xerrox Copy 1 Edvard GRIEG - Peer Gynt, Op 23 Ase's Death Sergei RACHMANINOV - Piano Concerto No. 2 Adagio Sostenuto (opening) Ryoji Ikeda - C0 : : Coda (For T.F.)
