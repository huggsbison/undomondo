The Notwist's main problem is this: they're boring. They're boring in the same way that Radiohead are boring. They represent, like the aforementioned coma inducers, Guardianista 'tastefulness'. They are the acceptable face of left-field indie. The avant-garde rendered palatable to Jools Holland's demographic. Their music is so pristine and perfect it's rendered sterile, lifeless, and utterly pointless. The soundtrack to a sixth form college art teacher's dinner party. I could never imagine any one – apart from maybe that art teacher– actually getting any enjoyment out of this record. Please, can we face some facts? Mum. Animal Collective. Efterklang. The Notwist. Fuck (right off) Buttons. They're all boring. They're all safe. And we should stop pretending they're anything but mediocre.
