Hype Machine watchlist 1

I'm late in figuring out the best way to keep up with mp3blogs : The Hype Machine Watchlist. Now it's pretty simple to keep track of my blogroll! Here's the first installment from the last two weeks. Is Vampire Weekend really worth the hype? Don't have the album but I've hunted some songs and they really seem to be fun. Check them out on Gorilla vs. Bear, Winnie Cooper, Allan's World of Music. 80's disco jazz from Don Cherry on Destination:Out. 70's US garage rock on Raven Sings the Blues. Feel the rock revival yo! Spaced out retro French synth-pop hit from Armand Duchien on ARAWA, huge Nigerian beat by Heavy Brothers Construction on Benn Loxo du Tacco, 60's soul from Jay & The Americans on Moistworks also check out the nudisco focus feat. Glass Candy and Kelley Polar.Also don't forget to listen to Habib Koite and Ennio Morricone on Motel de Moka, and here's my gift to the big family!.
