January 6, 2008 · 2008

Selections from the mp3blogosphere

* Samim - Heater (Diplo Remix) (Gee wizz) Diplo remix of Samim's 2007 anthem Heater. * Sonny Sharrock - Kula-Mae (Cyberinsekt) Jangling avant jazz/blues guitar. * Cloudland Canyon - Dambala *excerpt* (Press play, record) PPR : The first song, "Dambala," starts off with a drone, but it turns into a disturbing trip into space, with robotic creaks and glistening blasts of space. Then, halfway through the piece, with a jarring shift not unlike a primitive-era bone turning into a post-futuristic spaceship, the song turns into something different. A rather nice beat comes in, and keyboards are now prominently featured. Near the end of the piece, though, the sounds from the beginning fade back in, creating a lovely little sonic orchestra. * British Sea Power - Waving Flags (8/1). Mid paced epic indie rock from Rough Trade's British Sea Power. Not much to write home about though. * Burial - Untrue (Allan's World Music). At last someone sees through the Burial hype. The first Burial album was absolutely groundbreaking but the second one is not, it's just a good album nothing more. Tony Allen ending a lecture at Toronto University with a few minutes of drumming * Tony Allen - Love's a natural thing, Studio - Life's A Beach (Prins Thomas Remix) on and a Morgan Geist edit (Ugly Talented)
