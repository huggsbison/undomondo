Today's loot - 06.18

Some free mp3s I stumbled upon while surfing today; * Todd Sickafoose - Future Flora - West Coast * Todd Sickafoose - Bye Bye Bees Contemporary jazz which features Ani DiFranco and Andrew Bird among tons of other artists, buy from Indiejazz * Altar of Flies - Sometimes you can't wake up - Unholy Noise/drone from Sweden. * Talugung - Canal Side : Polyrhythmic Experimental stuff from Ontario, released last year on Foxglove. * Wayne Horvitz Gravitas Quartet - A Remembrance.. - ambient/classical/jazz, Horvitz releases on Tzadik. * Rabbit Girls - Across the Sky - noise / drone courtesy of Roil, a small, independent DIY label based in the Middle West, more tracks from Rabbit Girls and other artists like Ghoul Detail
