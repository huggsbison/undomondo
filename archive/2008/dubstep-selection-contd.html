Dub/Step Selection Cont'd

I received a mail from our fellow steppa head Golem, saying that the dubstep selection isn't complete without these tracks. Here are his oneliners and his selection. * Jakes : Nuff respect for Mr. Jakes for being one of the best dnb MCs around and now he steps into the dubstep scene as a producer. Big thingz! Label: Hench / * Vex'd : Everyone was waiting for this and finally it came out! This one tears down the place, big tune!! Label: Planet Mu / Boomkat * Distance : Distance once again shows that dubstep is not only for dancing. This is so deep and moody... Label : Chestplate / Boomkat PS: Updated the youtube blog with great videos from Lee Scratch Perry, Black Mountain from Pitchfork.tv, Holger Czukay, Pocahaunted, Kaki King, Soft Machine, Atlas Sound and Valet.
