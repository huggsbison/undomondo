June 22, 2008 · 2008

What the mailbox brought in June

Selection off the MP3s emailed to us by artists/labels & promotion companies we got sent this month.
  • Cof Cof - Caribbean Boy
  • Cof Cof - Forbidden Cocktail
    • Spaniards Cof Cof with their catchy lo-fi twee pop.
  • Tupolev - Reaset
    • Mödling's Tupolev has a gloomy sound, reminding me of Bohren & Der Club of Gore, Triosk and Tied and Tickled Trio, jazz meets post-rock and ambience.
  • Monsieur Meuble - I like your girlfriend
    • Frenchman Meuble presents his sample based electronica, inspired by Raymond Scott et al. Lovely song.
  • Pet Ghost Project - Age of Automatics
    • Third album from Brooklyn singer-songwriter Justin Stevers. Mature indierock with complex arrangements and fuzzy guitars a la 90's US noise rock. I wonder why he is barely listened on last.fm, too complex for the masses? Myspace
  • The Lodger - The Good Old Days
    • Catchy UK guitar pop on Slumberland Records. Myspace
  • The Hood Internet - Watch my big feet jump
    • Mash-up kings The Hood Internet give us a singalong track.
  • The Crystal Sun - Not all those that wander are lost (Dsico Remix)
    • Synth based Nudisco from Australia's Dsico, he's also released his Touch Me (Not Like That) album as (free download).
  • Steinski - Lesson 2 (James Brown Mix)
    • off the Steinski retrospective on Illegal Art.
