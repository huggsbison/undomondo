May 21, 2008 · 2008

Trus'me

One of the albums that didn't get as much attention as it deserved last year was "Working Nights" by Trus'me. Released in November 2007 by Manchester based producer David James Wolstencroft who has already garnered attention with his previous 12"s on LFM,"Working Nights" is a versatile cosmic dance album with roots in disco and soul, but also caters to the house (especially deep UK house) loving crowd as well. With the help of Gilles Peterson and his infamous ability to cause a commotion, Trus'me is actually pretty hot right now in cool dance circles. Moodymann lovers and Jazzanova crowd should better take notice as he's scheduled to work with Linkwood Family, Randolph, Amp Fiddler and Aaron Jerome during 2008.
