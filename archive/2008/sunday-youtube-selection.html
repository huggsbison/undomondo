May 4, 2008 · 2008

Sunday Youtube Selection

OvO - Live at Eli's Mile High Club in Oakland, CA I've been neglecting our youtube feed for some time (a month to be exact) yet I've bookmarked lots of videos in the meantime. I'm not posting them all at the same time though because I want you guys to watch & digest!. So today I have selected a bunch of live performances from Rapeman and Shellac, which will be performing in Istanbul on May 15. A short clip of a cheerful Steve Albini explaining what "Fake Italian" is, Tuxedomoon, Milano's OvO which I've discovered thanks to this video and some noise in the form of John Zorn, Pigs in the Ground and a Merzbow documentary. I used to post a lot of handpicked shit, so make sure you go back and watch them if you have time to burn.
