January 8, 2008 · 2008

Part 6 : Top 5 albums of 2007

Here's the last part, from now on new music!! Serbian Cocek 05 A Hawk and a Hacksaw and the Hun Hangár Ensemble - Self Titled (Leaf) : Together with the Bela Agostone lead Hun Hangar Ensemble, AHAAH has delivered a standardbearer neo-balkan-folk EP. Hope this collaboration results in more albums in the future.Past review on undomondo 04 High on Fire - Death Is This Communion (Relapse) : Oakland, CA's power stoner trio High on Fire's 10 ton riffage, is hands down the best & heaviest album for me. Metacritic. 03 Upsilon Acrux - Galapagos Momentum (Cuneiform) : One of the most extreme albums this year, Acrux' heavy prog-math rock is not everyone's cup of tea. Past review on undomondo 02 Marnie Stern - In Advance of the Broken Arm (Kill Rock Stars) : I can't believe how good this album turned out to be albeit being criticized for many critics as being gibberish, I'm highly impressed by her obsessive songwriting and shredding. Past review on undomondo Future of the Left - The Lord Hates A Coward (Live SXSW) 01 Future of the Left - Curses (Too Pure) : After careful consideration, I guess Mclusky survivors delivered the most complete album this year, arguably 2007's Entertainment!. Let's see if it will stand the test of time. Past review on undomondo Pitchfork review. Future of the Left interview. And so another list ends with the lyrics to Kept by Bees in case you wanna sing along. If anyone gets what they're talking about, msg me please. :)
