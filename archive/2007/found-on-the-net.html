June 3, 2007 · 2007

Found on the net

Marc Ribot * Well known avantgarde jazz guitarist Marc Ribot and other musicians were protesting the closing of world famous experimental / indie / avantgarde music venue Tonic, NYC by showing up for an improvised concert while the workers dismantled the stage!! Afterwards he was taken to station by the coppers!. Read more here and support your friends in NYC. Tonic, NYC group on Flickr. * Here's a label I found from comments on the above occassion. Engine Studios is a miniscule label that says no to dumbass music. Territories covered are avant jazz, afrobeat, world, jazz and good music in general, they sell their stuff for 6.50$, and if that isn't being generous, they've got a 20 song mp3 page with full tracks from all their acts. I only know the Kokolo Afrobeat Orchestra but I think I'm going to buy the Andrew Lamb Trio album right away, because spoken word on jazz seems to be one of my favourite indulgences right now. What Cheer? Brigade * What Cheer? Brigade is an 18-piece roving brass band that has played in trees, ferris wheels, zoos, grocery stores, bus stops, parties, farms, bars, libraries, soccer fields, cemetaries, and elementary school playgrounds since early 2005. * The Crisding Bladading is an experimental laptop noise improv project on La Fresto. 8 tracker free download called Split Ceremony on archive.org from 2004. * El Gallo Rojo Records is an Italian avantgarde jazz label with once again a generous free download page.
