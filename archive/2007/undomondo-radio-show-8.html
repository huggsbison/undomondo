March 15, 2007 · 2007

Undomondo Radio Show #8

I can't take the whole credit for this, thanks to my sucky mixing skills and an occasional bad apple. Not to bore you with our petty in-house dynamics, mersenne corrected my poor excuse for fade outs and squeezed in a song from his own vault. This time the set's more moodier than usual (at least the second half is) and it goes on and off between Polish bossa-nova, mechanic electronica, Turkish prairie folk and Japanese engineers. Enjoy the show if you haven't already enjoyed it on Dinamo.fm. alvin lucier - i am sitting in a room bossa nova combo - suliman galt mc dermot - fragments 1b nonato & seu conjunto - cafua asha puthli - right down here marc moulin - S.U.S bajka - vision (part 1-3) bernard hermann - diary of a taxi driver turlu tursu - yurukte yaylasi the cracow klezmer band - haniel pierre bastien - premier teleconcert moderato burnt friedman & jaki liebezeit - caracoles agf.3 & sue.c - everybody is a disaster pram - a million bubbles burst octet - trackball of fire rainstick orchestra - waltz for a little bird subtle - lay-z boy mountains - sewn one
