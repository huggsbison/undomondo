June 20, 2007 · 2007

Twitter update

I assume some of our readers are already twitter users. I haven't been a twitter freak really even if I originally thought it to be a nice application, until I saw Playtwitter, which is essentially the del.icio.us playtagger modded into a twitter player. It quickly dawned upon me to post updates via twitter and let people listen to music with one click, but soon, as all good things, I forgot it. Anyways today I finally realized this idea of publishing posts on twitter, if you're using firefox/greasemonkey just install the Playtwitter script to get fresh stuff daily via twitter with just one click.
