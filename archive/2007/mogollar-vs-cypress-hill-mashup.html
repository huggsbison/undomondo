Mogollar vs Cypress Hill Mashup

I've literally been a Cypress Hill fan back in the day and it won't be an exaggeration if I say that I can still successfully lip-sync to the first 3 albums. Although "Insane in the Brain" wasn't the high mark for me (The first and the third albums are much better) I've regained interest in the song, when one of my close friends from Istanbul did a mashup of the song with legendary Anatolian Rock band Mogollar (Les Moguls) for MTV Turkiye. The video and the song was widely sought by Turkish audiences and until today there was no option to download the song on the net. Thanks to being members of the same PES fellowship and a friendship history of about 10 years, we are the first site to offer you the download! Feedback appreciated!
