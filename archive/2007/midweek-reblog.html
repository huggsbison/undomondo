August 12, 2007 · 2007

Weekend Reblog

* As always don't miss the playlists from Motel de Moka. Specifically the one after Ingmar Bergmann's death, Moka's Shoegazing compilation and dangerous music including artists such as Raz Mesinai, Black Stalin, Motion Trio. * One of our favourite mp3blogs Needing to Be has a wealth of stuff like Roman Flugel's new album and other acts like, Magik Markers, Frisk Frugt, Richard Devine, High Places, Gang Gang Dance and more * Tunng's new one Bricks streamable from Last.FM reviewed on audiversity and obscure sound. * Covert Curiosity with tracks from Devandra's new one & Prinzhorn Dance School and Six Organs of Admittance. * Interesting post called "Seven Lee" including Lee Dorsey, Lee Scratch Perry, Lee Marvin etc. on Diddy Wah * Raven sings the Blues has found another artist similar to our past love Alela Diane. Nevada singer songwriter Marie Sioux just has her debut on the Grass Roots label. * One track from the new Madlib Beat Kondukta CD on Nialler9. * One track from the new Akron/Family album Phenomena on Rawking Refuses to Stop. * Isolee - Bleu (Dixon Remix) on Can you see the Sunset's electronic mixtape. * Little White Ear Buds July mix, mostly minimal house/techno like Andy Stott, on other posts Jahcoozi, Kerri Chandler * Mohawk Lodge on Rewriteable Content. * Sharity love on Bravo Juju, two hard to find records. An Ali Akhbar Khan album from 2002 & Miracle of Levitation : Experimental sounds from the US and Japan compilation. * Grindcore via Relapse Records incl. Leng T'che, Regurgitate, Pig Destroyer and my new fave Coldworker. Thanks to Lupatarkastaja.
