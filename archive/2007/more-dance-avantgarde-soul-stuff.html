June 4, 2007 · 2007

More dance : Avantgarde Soul Stuff

Micro photography by Appie Bonis The second compilation from Barcelona's Lasal label formed after the beach & open-air bar of the same name (since 1993!) showcases a selection from high quality contemporary urban dance music that none of us with a sane mind can resist. A range of styles like urban soul, reggae, deep house, leftfield hiphop, broken beats, electronica are apparent. Of course avid listeners of these styles may notice the fact that this compilation is from 2006 and has some well-known hits like Recloose's Dust and Isolee's Cardiology Remix, the real treat here was the opener by Trinityroots and Mark de Clive-Lowe's Slide, with its great slide bass at the end of the track.
