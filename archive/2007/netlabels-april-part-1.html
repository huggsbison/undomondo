April 27, 2007 · 2007

Netlabels April Part 1

I've skimmed through a lot of netlabel releases this month. Most of them are minimal house/techno/electro tools which I have no interest in yet still I have lots of quality & free music for you people. Part 2 tomorrow. the cover to Karl-Johan Nilsson's release on One
Tzii - bienvenue a la violence (antisocial) A great opener, "welcome to violence a noisy disturbing opener. Jan von Grafenstein - Chi (Test Tube) Takes samples from Fela Kuti's Lady and uses it in a very different way, postmodern sampling! Jan von Grafenstein - September 23 Authist & Dub One! - His royal Highness (Ideology) mellow & melodic dub with flute work. Karl-Johan Nilsson - Someg (One) Karl-Johan Nilsson - Cloud 8 (One) minimal textures with scratches and glitches from Canada's OneeXperience #09 (bumpfoot) Melodic triphop Uniquetunes - Eggs Of Jazz (Bump foot) Uniquetunes - Bitch City Grooves (Bump foot) Very interesting song, my pick of the bunch, strings & sax but with a strange surreal feel. Lukeshara - Glistenia (Nuten) Melodic & percussive electro house BUBEN - Empire builders (Clinical Archives) A brooding dark ambient piece which feels like a march of the empire builders.
