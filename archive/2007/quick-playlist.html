November 7, 2007 · 2007

Quick playlist

After some protests for not updating undomondo, here's a quick playlist selected from what I've been listening to recently. Electronica artist Odd Nosdam aka David Madson, who recently released a new album called "Level Live Wires" on Anticon. Lovely guitar folk from Turner Cody, experimental electronics meet with ambience & trumpet by Australian Roam the Hello Clouds and an old track reminded me by a friend yesterday by Os Carrascos, featured on Senor Coconut's 2005 compilation Legendary Latin Club Tunes. More to follow soon :)
Odd Nosdam - Up in Flames Turner Cody - Forever Forever Roam the Hello Clouds - Phases Os Carrascos - Labirinto dos Carrasco
