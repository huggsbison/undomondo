June 25, 2007 · 2007

Burial

Eraserhead + Burial ! Hi kids, been away for the weekend, and did my first dip in water this year!!. One of the releases I took with me was the eagerly anticipated new release by the mysterious Burial on UK's Hyperdub which had a very fruitful year in 2006. This new 12" called Ghost Hardware is prime time Burial stuff a la his debut. All of the 3 tracks are dominated by his signature dark reverby sound and broken beats, and his restless non progressing compositions. More of the stuff that we've come to know. Now I love his unquestionably unique sound and his layered compositions but I always feel kinda alarmed about "more of the same old stuff". Hopefully there'll be more variety and experimentation on his next album.
