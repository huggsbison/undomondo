Undomondo Radio Show #39 - Happy Holidays

Hola! If you're wondering where on earth shows #33-38 are, don't! #39 was a special one where I set out to do an alternative show from christmas songs but failed. The first part begins with some great christmas tunes, goes into some oldschool hiphop & booty bass and finally ends with a superb Turkish folk jazz piece, which I found through 45devir.net thanks to our reader Henri!. All three monotheistic religions had holidays around the same time, and although I'm not religious at all, I'd like to say happy holidays to everyone please don't hurt each other and coexist dudes.
