April 4, 2007 · 2007

Mp3blog roundup 04.04.07

Today I'm being an mp3blog selecta! Here's some lovely music that you should be listening to instead of that new buzz band! * Farben - Live At Sahara Tahoe, 1973 (Little White Earbuds) : glitchy electronica * Differnet - Perpetual Summer (Homemade Avantgarde) : ambient/textural * Mou, lips! - looking for symmetry in the laws of nature (Homemade Avantgarde) : Once again from the "On how a picture can sound" release * Stars of the Lid - And their refinement of the decline (Shameless Complacency) Ambient / from their new release. Stars of the Lid & Eluvium - Heaven Water Fire Earth * Herbert - Back to the Start (Brooklyn Vegan) : Might be my fave Herbert track, anticipating his new release Score. * 120 days - SXSW (woxyblog "The Futurist".) : electropop * 50 Hertz - Ring of Fire (My old Kentucky Blog) : electropop / MOKB's covers project has over 15 "Ring of Fire" covers, this is by far the funniest cover by Swedish mockelectro band 50 Hertz. * MF DOOM - Elite Scooby Doo Shit (Gorilla vs. Bear) : hiphop / rare MF Doom piece. * Redman ft. Method Man - Blow Treez (The Passion of the Weiss) : hiphop / been reading mixed reviews about his new album, but this is some good dubby shit! Redman interviewed on High Times potcast. * Polish Jazz Quartet - Przechadzka Pusstymi Ulicami (Waxidermy) : jazz / quality retro Polish jazz * Jumbonics - Bubble Drop (Aurgasm) : downtempo & nujazz / great piece by leftfield hop crew Jumbonics. * Rare 80's hiphop 12" rips from Stretch Armstrong's blog Konstant Kontakt : hiphop * Weekend Steve Mix by Allez-Allez (alainfinkielkrautrock) : rare retro disco mix / YSI be quick. Tommy Seebach Band - Apache * Ni Hao! - Wiper (tenzenmen) : spazz punk / if you liked afrirampo * Ni Hao! - Romantic Scratch (Tenzenmen) : spazz punk / you'll like these japanese all girl trio. * ILiKETRAiNS - Citizen (The Daily Growl) : postrock / instrumental rock Korpi Ensemble - Like Father Like Son * Ted Leo & The Pharmacists - Live at The Boston Opera House Full Set (Bradley's Almanac) : indie rock / A full live set from 2006. * Born Against - Self Titled EP (7 inch punk) : hardcore punk / I reject the corporate sellout. * A spoonful of Emo (Can you see the sunset) : emo hardcore / now this is a great history lesson to all the new wave emo kids and/or emo haters on what the first/second wave emo was about featuring 39 tracks from Braid, Jejune, Get up kids, Hot Water Music, Mineral, Knapsack, Sunny Day Real Estate, Jawbreaker, Cap N Jazz, Nation of Ulysses, Rites of Spring, Fugazi and more. Salivating! To:read * Alan Vega interview (Alainfinkielkrautrock) * The April edition of Textura magazine with around 50 reviews. * Watch VBS.TV, the official tv of Vice guys, with great micro music / street life documentaries * Wired on P2P and music economy. * Microtonal Music on Wikipedia.
