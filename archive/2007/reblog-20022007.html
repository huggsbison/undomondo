February 20, 2007 · 2007

Reblog 20.02.2007

Great photos from indie music photographer Jeff Winterberg who's recently got a book of his previous stuff * Environ's Daniel Wang interviews Italo Disco Pioneer Daniele Baldelli (Cosmic Disco) on Discopia, a mag on disco/neo-disco. 9 issues online also read The Emperor Machine Q&A, Alexander Robotnick interview, and also an interview with disco/house pioneer Nicky Siano of Studio 54. Great mag kids. * d-i-r-t-y - mixed by guillaume sorge (39.5mb, 28:48mins) via allez-allez tracklist available * top 10 gig posters quality selection of gig posters which you can buy, incl. acts like raconteurs, silver jews, built to spill. * RJD2 Pre-Mix community on IMEEM. RJD2 has all the samples ready for you to premix a song. Premix? Yeah it's an innovative experiment which gives you the readers to actually create a song from hits/stabs before you hear the actual song. Go nuts! * Jazz between 73-90, all the best stuff, gathered by Ethan Iverson of Do the Math blog.
