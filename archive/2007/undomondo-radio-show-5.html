February 21, 2007 · 2007

Undomondo Radio Show #5

But Ferdinand would shake his head. "I like it better here where I can sit just quietly and smell the flowers." Well I'm back at the helm for the latest installment of our chaotic radio show, this time with somehow a more dubby vibe. You can either get it from the archive.org page (if you'd like to listen as stream or download ogg or at a different quality) or download directly from below. We have some moog covers by Jean-Jacques Perrey and later on The Moog Cookbook, cheerful Brasilian vibes from Nublu's Forro in the Dark (which I'll be featuring very soon), an interesting break where I played my favourite song from Terre Thaemlitz' Lovebomb, which features the African National Congress' declaration of war against the Apartheid regime played on top of The Plants' textures and Krepulec's martial piece. From then on we have a party vibe going with some urban hiphop/2step followed by dub and more dub, the last two tracks are special, the first is by Baba Zula one of the prominent turkish bands that you've got to know if you like dub, and the closer is a gem I found on a compilation called Zanzibara 2 (1965-1975 Golden years of Mombasa Taarab). Don't forget to tune in for the next episode which will be aired this Saturday 10AM CET @ dinamo.fm, which will be selected by my co-pilot Error101. Here's the tracklist. 01. zain bhikha - here we come allah 02. jean-jacques perrey & dana countrymen - hernando's hideaway 03. erik satin - magnifique 04. forro in the dark - forrowest 05. hildegard knef - heute morgen war termin (andreas dorau remix) 06. the plants - umpid pool of time 06. terre thaemlitz - between empathy and sympathy is time 06. krepulec - independent song 07. man or astroman? - espante del fuero 08. cypress hill - something for the blunted 09. diplo and dr. evil - mary jane 10. m.i.a - bucky done gun 11. geiom - feel so bad (feat. terrible shock) 12. fat freddy's drop - cays crays (digital mystikz remix) 13. kanka - nova dub 14. jimpster - left and right (feat. capitol a) 15. 12me & raph - jazz session 16. dat politics - senior actif 17. keziah jones - kpafuca 18. dekoboko hajime (john zorn) & yamantaka eye - eep man 19. the moog cookbook - sweet home alabama 20. white magic - song of solomon 21. rhythm & sound - dem never know (feat. jah cotton) 22. baba zula - fayiman cuneyt 23. yasseen mohamed - ndege kaa ufikiri
