April 8, 2007 · 2007

Reblog 04.08.2007

* Parenthetical Girls and The Dead Science on Brooklyn Vegan * Huge Zappa feature on Feed me good tunes. * Oren Ambarchi, Lichens, Horseback and Talibam! a great selection of tracks from upcoming noise/drone/ambient/processing albums on Paper Thin Walls. * 2007 Psych/Drone/Freak Thread on ILX * 2 new blogs added to linkroll : Bumrocks and Lovefingers, featuring single tracks that are mostly old classics and disco edits, crazy hard to find tracks and niche blogs!. Stuff from Neil Young, Kid Creole, Mort Garson, Can, Donald Byrd on LF and Marcos Valle, Cal Tjader, Gong, Time Bandits on BF. * Lovely historical house music feature on SNUH, featuring tracks from artists like Frankie Knuckles and Ten City and original acid housers Phuture. * New cover blog on the block. Coverfreak has Richard Cheese, Telly Savalas and Enoch Light among others.
