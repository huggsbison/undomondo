April 20, 2007 · 2007

Undomondo Radio Show #12

bighappyfunhouse Here's #12 mixed by yours truly. Mad love to the Galapagos4 crew for Deejay OM and Mike Gao albums. Dhafer Youssef for a oud meets west ethnic jazz masterpiece, especially the part after the breakdown around 2nd minute is totally spectacular. I've mentioned the Nostalgia 77 album which is topnotch, and hopefully gonna talk about it more next week. Right in the middle of the mix, there's a special Turkish jazz song by Mustafa Ozkent remastered and released by Mr. Andy Votel of Finders Keepers. R.I.P Dilla. D-D-Dirty Diggers. Back to the past with Squarepusher's My Red Hot Car and Deerhoof's Strawberry Bananas. Love Marnie. Don't forget to tune in tomorrow at 10AM CET for our next show. Listen Live @ Dinamo.FM.
