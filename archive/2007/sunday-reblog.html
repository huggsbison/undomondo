December 9, 2007 · 2007

Sunday Reblog

After a long period of overlooking them I've cleaned up some of our links and checked out some mp3blogs. * Sir Richard Bishop of Sun City Girls, Live video courtesy of Eat Tapes * Favourite Songs of 2007 and Dan Deacon live coverage on Nialler9 * Mahshav by John Zorn's Masada Chamber Ensembles on Los Amigos de Durutti * A sad note by Inq Magazine informs us that Karlheinz Stockhausen has passed away. R.I.P. One piece on Brooklyn Vegan, two others on Funeral Pudding. * Rudy Giuliani 9/11 Speech Remix Contest on WFMU!!! What do you say, will he win the Presidency?! That would really mean the end of the world is nigh! * Linda Perhacs on FluoKids * Great stuff on Innersounds, Cymande, Akwabaa, Todd Terje and to seal the deal, Pharoah Sanders, whom I watched live with his quartet yesterday. Long live the Pharoah!
