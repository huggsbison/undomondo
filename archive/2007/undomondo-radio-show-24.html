September 6, 2007 · 2007

Undomondo Radio Show #24

For every balkan & klezmer fan out there, this one is specially for you. It's not one of my best shows i must admit, but i've been trying to tone the musical variety down in these last shows, attempting to make more wholesome shows and i hope in time i'll get better at it. It's not like i've been filling one half with balkan and the other with jewish though, i tried to include sub or mid genre pieces as well and different stuff that would go along with the rest, like Secret Chiefs, Mr.Bungle, Zakarya or Rashanim, most of whom reside in previous undomondo reviews and radio shows. I think anyone who's had enough of both genres will understand. God knows i had enough, i'll probably be going easy on the balkan stuff in the recent future. available from archive.org as stream and odeo as podcast.
