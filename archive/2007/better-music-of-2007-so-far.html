May 9, 2007 · 2007

Better music of 2007 so far

Image scanned from a French fashion magazine from 1977 by Martijn Konijn. The first 4 months have gone by and indeed 2007 has been lucrative for music. As the collective memory is so low these days and there's so much production and hype, I want to emphasize the better stuff once more. Here's a short recollection of what I've been listening to these past months, I couldn't listen to lots of 2007 stuff thoroughly, but you can bet that we'll be covering the latest efforts from Colleen, Stars of the Lid, De-Phazz, Kammerflimmer Collective, Badgerlore, Antibalas, The Focus Group, Pole, Islaja, Tied and Tickled Trio, Low, Panda Bear, Cocorosie, Hauschka, Miguel Migs, Thief, Solo Moderna, The Cinematic Orchestra and more when we get to listen to them.
Marnie Stern - Patterns of a Diamond Ceiling (review) Alamaailman Vasarat - Katkorapu (review) Field Music - In Context (review) Men-an-Tol - Orange Juice and Vodka (review) Nostalgia 77 - Dreamers Dance (review) Deejay OM - The Lullaby of the Nighttime in Cairo (review) Blonde Redhead - Signs along the Path (review) Punkt - Bird's Eye Raw Artistic Soul - The Light (featuring Ursula Rucker) (review) The Berg Sans Nipple - Of the Sung (review)
