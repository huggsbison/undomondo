November 27, 2007 · 2007

Undomondo Radio Show #30

Image by Mehdi Saeedi Another milestone show, #30! Feels like we've been doing Undomondo forever, when it's barely 30 (it's already 35 btw, you know I'm slow to keep up with it online). Starts of folky with acts like Hala Strana & Golden Arm Trio, Early Songs, Luke Temple, Jens Lekman, amazing songs! The second part of the show is more jazzy, loungy stuff, I remember being particularly hooked on Delgui's broken beat house track, which is the opener of their new album. The end bit has some rock from FotL and orchestral jazz from Kerkko Koskinen.
