Made In Sweden - Snakes In A Hole

made in sweden performing "Our man" live. Another one from the dusty cabinet, Snakes In A Hole is an LP from '69. Founded in '67, Made In Sweden trio was probably the first prog-rock band in Sweden and also one of the pioneering prog bands throughout the world, since prog-scene wasn't so heavy until around 1970. This album has a different sound than other hard-core prog bands, being very jazzy & bluesy while also being progressive and it had been out of print for decades and never issued on cd until a 2005 reissue.
