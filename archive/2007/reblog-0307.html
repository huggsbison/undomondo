July 3, 2007 · 2007

Reblog 03.07

by [windscreen fly] Haven't been checking thru what the other mp3blogs have been up to for a long while, today I tried to check back using hype machine but i could only stroll through 3 days or so because there was too much stuff!! Anyways, here's a quality selection from today's session and some more stuff I previously labeled as `feature` : * Sahar Belaya Smert - In the Ice Madleen (WFMU) around 20 retro ukranian punk/surf/nowave stuff * Donkey Monkey - Phoolan Devi (Glubibulga) * DVA - Harom Kerom (Glubibulga) Glubibulga seems to be my favourite radioshow for now! Great taste, In fact I'm inclined to check through all their compilations perfect!!! * Pizzicato Five - Twiggy Twiggy (I am fuel, you are friends) Pizzicato's best known track ever? * Younnat - Doctor Verhovtsev's Dream (bumpfoot) * Younnat - The Day After Tomorrow (bumpfoot) From Younnat's synth electronica & IDM album on the Bumpfoot netlabel, reminded me of Belbury Poly * Picastro - Albonis (Kwaya Na Kisser) Kwaya Na Kisser features tracks from the latest Picastro album! * Bonde de Role - Geremia (Blogs are for dogs) * Basia Bulat - I Was A Daughter (Blogs are for dogs) From their best of 2007 list, Bonde de Role's baile funk and indie pop with female vocals by Basia Bulat. * War - Galaxy (20 jazz funk greats) * Art Ensemble Of Chicago - Theme de Yoyo (20 jazz funk greats) If you're going to click only one link from this selection make it this one, juicy afrobeat & jazz & disco stuff! * Medeski Martin And Wood - Mami Gato (Motel de Moka) * Milt Jackson & John Coltrane - Be-Bop (Motel de Moka4) Another one of those Motel de Moka selections by Squashed, loved these 2 jazz tracks but there's lots more to discover * John Cage - Mureau (via This Recording) A recording of John Cage reading a poem by Thoreau and some rare albums on the sharity blog Babe(b)logue: Rabih Abou-Khalil & Balanescu Quartet , Boris Kova? & Ladaaba Orchestra\\5 and the compilation Choubi Choubi!
