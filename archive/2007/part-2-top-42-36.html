December 19, 2007 · 2007

Part 2 : Top 42-36

Les Savy Fav - Let's stay Friends (Frenchkiss) : So I submitted to indie hypster art rock shit with the Brooklyn indierockers Les Savy Fav. Their uptempo indie stomp is catchy as well, filling in the shoes of last years Bloc Party for me. Pjusk - Sart (12K): Barren drony soundscapes from Norway by the duo of Jostein Dahl Gjelsvik and Rune Sagevik. Marbert Rocel - Speed Emotions (Compost): Haven't seen this album mentioned anywhere, German electropop band Marbert Rocel's debut shone among hundreds of cliche electronic pop albums. Good songwriting and happy moods. Adjágas - Adjágas (Ever Records): Sami Yoiksters Adjágas with a folk album full of folk tales and chanting. Past review on undomondo. Wahoo - Take It Personal (Fine Records): Berlin based Wahoo is euro house veterans George Levin and Dixon. Their debut album includes their past hit "Make 'em shake it" alongside lots of hip club/dance tunes. Euro-hipness at its best. The Berg Sans Nipple - Along the Quai (Team Love): Past review on undomondo. Tied and Tickled Trio - Aelita (Morr Music): Melancholic and cinematic album by the Munich based Tied and Tickled Trio. Past review on undomondo.
