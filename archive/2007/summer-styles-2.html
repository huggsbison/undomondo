May 13, 2007 · 2007

Summer Styles 2

cartoon from a beautiful revolution Here's Part 2 of our deep house selection for summer, including the funk induced dancer by Move D on Compost, as opposed to his usual minimalistic style, Marvin Belton's deep summer tune from 2005, which is a appropriately titled an Early AM Mix, Dennis Ferrer's hot tribal rhythms spiced with pizzicato strings and fab accordeon work, Broken Compass' raw aboriginal disco dancer with pebbles as percussions and a cheerful african tune from percussionist Lekan Babalola, which is not really house music, but will no doubt move your hips.
Broken Compass - Australia (Main Mix) Dennis Ferrer - Destination (Original Mix) Move D - Your Rolling Hills Marvin Belton - Any Day Now (Early AM Mix) Lekan Babalola - Aso Kere
