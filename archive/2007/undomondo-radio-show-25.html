September 7, 2007 · 2007

Undomondo Radio Show #25

Sun shines on the Guggenheim museum in NYC #25. Is the second show we've done with Cleansweep. In fact it's the same session cut in two. If you remember #23 it's just like that, roots dub, jazz, funk, nu jazz, beat, afrobeat selected by Can Ayverdi and yours truly. BTW I'm trying to redesign the blog hopefully in a better readable way, so excuse us having less posts and less words per posts than usual. also available for stream @ archive.org and podcast at odeo [podcast]http://www.archive.org/download/Undomondo_Radio_Show_25/undomondo--radio_show_25.mp3[/podcast]
