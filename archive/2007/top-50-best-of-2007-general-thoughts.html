Top 50 Best of 2007 - General thoughts

Image by Kristian Olson What's up boys and girls, this year I'm a little bit faster in compiling the best of list (although still one of the last to do so according to Largehearted Boy's best of lists compilation.) 2007 was very very lucrative for new music and even a top 50 list isn't enough to cover the noteworthy albums. I'll publish the top 50 list now and gradually go through them in the coming days with an mp3 for every album, till the end of this year. As always my subjective rating algorithm is mostly based on inventiveness with respect to other artists & bands + personal taste. I've listened to a lot of experimental & improv stuff as well, but as I look back they are mostly not distinctive or well-formed enough to end in a top 50 list, but that doesn't make them any less good!
