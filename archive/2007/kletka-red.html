July 7, 2007 · 2007

Kletka Red

a 'video clip' of Kletka Red, made from stuff filmed on the bands last tour. Mostly in Venice. Do you like Klezmer music? Are you also a fan of high intensity in your face speed punk metal? Then this album is for you. Kletka Red's "Hijacking" came out in 1996 on John Zorn's Tzadik label. It's Klezmer on crank. The band is Leonid Soybelman, Andy Moor (Dog Faced Hermans, Ex), Tony Buck, and Joe Williamson. From what I've read they took traditional Klezmer songs and turned them into high powered metal. The first tracks remind me of Melt Banana and have no discernable reference to Klezmer. Other tracks definitely have the flavor. Check it out. I really enjoyed this album. Here are two tracks for your enjoyment and wish fulfilment. Mazel tov! Mersenne's note : The first song seems to be the traditional Greek Dance you can check it out here, both of our freehosts seem to be down for the time being, at least they don't work for me.
