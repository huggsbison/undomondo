April 28, 2007 · 2007

Netlabels April Part 2

Here's part 2 of the netlabel selection for april. A little bit more dance oriented this time.
Tzii - ça fait marcher le monde (antisocial) 7am & family groove - collective (insectorama) Dubby minimal floorfiller. markus masuhr - hamptington liips (insectorama) Groovy & loose minimal driver by Masuhr. Phour Trakk - Sleeping City (Ideology) Downtempo/triphop with grainy soundscapes, sax & ambience, suits the title. Gozne - La botica (Bump foot) Chilean Gozne with a catchy melody on top of a laidback latin american rhythm. Zavoloka - Lychko Rumjanilo (telescope) Vitalis Popoff - [Tristia] (telescope) 2 cold minimal ambient tracks from the first Telescope compilation. Phelios & C.Stritzel - Schweifend (tonatom) Ambient atmospheric soundscapes on Tonatom
