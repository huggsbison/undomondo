June 21, 2007 · 2007

Undomondo Radio Show #19

image nicked from Tiny Vices Once again we're behind schedule in releasing our shows online. Here's #19 done by yours truly, it's a journey from electronic to mechanic to african & brasil jazz and ends with ethnic music from Balkans and Turkey. There's some funny overdubs by grindcore heads Gut, I can't play grindcore on the show but no one gets hurt with some samples. I also love "My beer" by David Shrigley, which was also the creator for the interludes of our first shows. You can stream from archive.org or subscribe as a podcast on odeo. (overdub) gut - back in your back apparat - not a number battles - race : in gangpol und mit - overture pascal comelade & pierre bastien & jaki liebzeit & jac beccoral - to the last of imaginary solutions emil richards - amethyst andrew hill - without malice pekka streng - puutarhassa vanessa da mata - quando um homem tem uma mangueira no quintal os sambeatles - i should have known better lukid - fela rhythm funk masters - nudinuff mardi grass.bb - herb cooky dj vadim - black is the night feat. katherin deboer patchworks and mr. day - brothers on the slide (the dynamics edit) (overdub) gut - can't wait for tonight feat. beyonce bowels late night tales present shrigley - my beer a hawk and a hacksaw & the han hungar ensemble - ihabibi richard galliano - luz negra senem diyici quartet - abe lalo
