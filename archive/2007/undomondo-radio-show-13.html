Undomondo Radio Show #13

Oh, we're so late, tomorrow's our #15th program, yet on the net we're only on the #13th. I'll try to fix it up in the coming days. Here's a mix by error101, it's a playful mix of music from all around the world & time (but not the washed up "World Music" with capital letters.) . India, Japan, Balkans, Disco, Aerobic whatever it's all here. Especially bummed with Zalatnay Sarolta who's got a reissue this year, check it out if you're into retro bizarre stuff. also available as stream from archive.org and odeo 01. andy votel - gentleman thief (ft. the ged hone big four) 02. gordon jenkins - good bye 03. asha boshie rd borman - piya too ab to aaja 04. mohammed rafi - jan pahechan ho 05. the tornados - the gremmie part 1 06. shugo tokamaru - paparazzi 07. bibio - the cranking house 08. zakarya - charleston 09. tummel - leben zol palestina 10. zalatnay sarolta - megmondtak elore 11. kila - rusty nails 12. vytas brenner - dorado ii 13. richard hayman - gris gris 14. nino nardini & roger roger - shere khan 15. galt mac dermot - tango 16. arp life - jalousie 17. alain goraguer - gym-tonic (echauffment)
