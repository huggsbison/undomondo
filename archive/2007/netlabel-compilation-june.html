June 17, 2007 · 2007

Netlabel Compilation June

Here's a quick compilation gathered from around 10 netlabel releases I've checked this month, Some of the high notes are the Takeshi Nakamura experimental electronics on Zymogen , oldschool IDM happy breakcore by Mithra on Weird and Wired, dark textural noise & ambience by Mike Hansen & Tomasz Krakowiak on Frozen Elephants and the Tripostal compilation from a few months ago featuring one of our favourite netlabel artists Bacanal Intruder and most of the Carte Postale gang. Make sure you get the complete releases if you like the chosen tracks.
