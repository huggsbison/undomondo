September 6, 2007 · 2007

Nishi / Camomille / Bumpfoot

Here's a quick selection of new stuff from some of our favourite netlabels : Nishi : Cagey House's Dave Keifer is back once again with his trademark synths. Exotic and atonal melodies & shifting beats and sometimes downright spooky melodies! Who'd want to be inside that "Law Office", I know I don't.! Rich.vom.Dorf. is of the oldschool IDM & Melodic Electronica tradition, while Canadian Holzkopf has the greatest songtitles like "Katrina Make Us a Zombie Town" and "The First 17 Minutes After Death" which is actually a quite suitable picturing of Death!. Diverse album with elements of noise/breakcore/drones and more noise. Camomille : Camomille's Pocka is Kikapu netlabel owner Brad Mitchell, who comes along with soft but unsettling ambient soundscapes. While Dague de miséricorde's Dérobé is "a 3 track forray into improvisation". Interesting highly unstructured violin & noise improvisations creating a tense atmosphere. There's also one more track in the mix from 2006's Photophob EP which sounds like old Rephlex IDM.
