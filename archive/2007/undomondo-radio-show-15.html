Undomondo Radio Show #15

A young Bill Cosby Ok once again I forgot to add the previous radioshows, this website unfortunately takes too much of my time and my colleagues never help me and you guys never comment as well!! Anyways, here's error101's #15 where she goes all the ways to the roots, and plays jazz & beats and all kinds of weird 70s stuff including Letta Mbulu's unforgettable "What is wrong with groovin'", Kathleen Emery's Sometimes I feel like a motherless child and Horace Silver's Cape Verdean Blues. She's playing tomorrow @ 9AM CET, and 11AM local time on Dinamo.FM 103.8, don't forget to listen if you're up at that godawful time. you can stream from archive.org or subscribe to odeo podcast. 01. billy cosby talks to the kids about drugs - dope pusher 02. johnny pearson - thunderbird 03. som tres - tanga 04. cymande - equitorial forest 05. wavering - roy porter 06. kathleen emery - sometimes i feel like a motherless child 07. dee felice trio - nightingale 08. horace silver - cape verdean blues 09. lorez alexandria - send in the clowns 10. rabbinical school dropouts - semitic slam 11. orgone - funky nassau 12. letta mbulu - what is wrong with groovin' 13. sidewinder - ego riot 14. jean jacques perrey & dana countryman - trolls story 15. daedelus - something bells 16. jimi tenor - vexations
