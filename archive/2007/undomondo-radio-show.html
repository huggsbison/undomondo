January 25, 2007 · 2007

Undomondo Radio Show #1

Something I forgot to tell you guys, because I was dealing with the Hirant Dink murder on this weekend, was the Undomondo radioshow that began to be aired on the biggest electronic & indie radio station in Istanbul, Dinamo.FM 103.8. I was asked to do a show which reflected the freestyle approach of this mp3blog, and I'm pretty happy with my seeming (in)ability to mix stuff together. Here's the tracklist of the first show which will be aired on every Saturday @ 10AM CET. As I got told by a radio insider (!), the response was pretty hip at least inside the radio, which is hopefully a good thing. I want to release these shows as mp3s, but I need your advice on a reliable podcasting service, odeo or archive.org or good old rapidshare? I don't know yet, till then just enjoy the tracklist which mostly features some older stuff I reviewed here mixed with some gems, and let me remind you that you can tune in to Dinamo.FM 103.8 from the internet and they have a pretty good sound quality as well. 01.
