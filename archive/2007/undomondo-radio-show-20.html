July 15, 2007 · 2007

Undomondo Radio Show #20

Image by Mirko Zander Once again I've been pretty slow in uploading the radioshows, for some reason a week's time is never enough to upload it in time, but this time the delay has been gross. Anyways I've upped all the shows on archive as of today and I'll be upping it one at a time this week! #20 features, a lot of leftfield hiphop from UK's Dirty Diggers, J. Rawls & Holmskillit, Kidkanevil even some neo-oldschool stuff by Jazzy Jeff. Some crazy stuff from Peru and Japan, particularly the awesome instrumental version of Otemoyan by Teruo Yoda and the japanese dub track by Accidental's Setsubun Bean Unit. Interlude's are from The Focus Group's new album "We are all Pan's People" and the closing oriental dub piece is off Fedayi Pacha's new one "The 99 Names of Dub". available as podcast from odeo or stream from archive.org dj twelve - in trouble last soul descendents - eclipse n'dambi - rain dirty diggers - don't know about that j. rawls & holmskillit - still with me the focus group - hang along teruo yoda & 6 lemons - otemoyan setsubun bean unit - rettsu kissu nicomedes santa cruz - no me cumben kidkanevil - click click pop the focus group - look hear now dj jazzy jeff - all i know (ft. cl smooth) kidkanevil - water sign gare du nord - chets chat john coltrane - bahia sayag jazz machine - distante ya fedayi pacha - yallah cowboy
