Reblog 08.07

Art by Amy Sol from the Aidan Savoy Gallery * Fabchannel.com is a videosharing site for high quality full concert videos, including artists like Oxbow, Do Make Say Think, You say party! we say die!, Isis, Forro in the Dark, Bebel Gilberto, Comets on Fire, Spank Rock, Oi va Voi, Boris, Benni Hemm Hemm and about a hundred or so more! * Black Soul - Mangous Ye on Chez Lubacov. Not to be missed soulful african disco groove from 76!. * Loudersoul takes us back to the disco era with Sergio Mendes & Brasil '88's "I'll Tell You" * Afrobeat selection by the Butter Team, including Fela & Femi Kuti and King Sunny Ade. * Telefon Tel Aviv remix to Ammoncontact's BBQ Plate on Comfort Music. * Commentary on SF and a Paperclip People classic on SOAP * Henryk GÄ´recki - Symphony 3 on Sonorama * Mapsadaisical reviews of the recent Jodi Cave and Pjusk releases on Taylor Deuprees'12K label * 2 tracks by Finland weirdfolkers Kemialliset Ystävät on our friend Huxtable's Needing to Be * Warped Reality featuring the psychfolk label Secret Eye including tracks by Black Forest/Black Sea, Larkin Grimm and Spires That In The Sunset Rise and an interview with label boss Miriam Goldberg. * 2 tracks by Icelandic indie rockers Benni Hemm Hemm on Manifatturiere Broz * And finally for the jazzheads Wordsandmusic has songs from Cecil Taylor, Anthony Braxton and Ornette Coleman.
