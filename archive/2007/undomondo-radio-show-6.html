Undomondo Radio Show #6

Greetings to all undo citizens! after a long term of pause and neglect on my part, i present you part 6 of our crazy little show. As the intro puts it, i'm just glad to be on the show and i hope these shows will eventually rob you of those precious comments you hold on so tight:) enjoy the tracklist and have a nice day. You can also get ogg vorbis, a 64k version or stream from the archive.org page. de la soul - intro universal robot band - disco trek arp life - besame mucho triste janero - samba de orphia forro in the dark feat. miho hatori - paraiba steve beresford - couleur cafe medeski, martin & wood - intoxicated man judy nylon - jailhouse rock the dance - do dada oscar sulley and the uhuru dance band - bukom mashie glenn kotche - fantasy on a shona theme okkyung lee - that undeniable empty feeling oddjob - bigfoot kobol - trio in a box kazi - a.v.e.r.a.g.e madlib - pikes strike funkaho - bootay
