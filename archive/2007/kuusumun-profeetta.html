March 31, 2007 · 2007

Kuusumun Profeetta

Have you noticed the recent surge of Finnish music on undomondo? I did and that led me to mention another Finnish band, Kuusumun Profeetta, the Moon Fog Prophet. I've first heard their name on a compilation called Jazzflora from 2004. Although the comp had lovely music from the likes of Hird, Five Corners Quintet & Kahuun, Profeetta's brushed downtempo opener Kovin Lentäen Kotiin Kaipaanon had rendered me unable to listen to the remaining songs. Last year they had an EP out on Fonal, which I jumped over, however I couldn't find the jazzy vibes I fell in love with but saw it replaced by more theatrical progressive elements with even more Finnish vocals. Anyways, while I was writing the previous post, I gave a second listen to the 4 tracker and loved the closing track with the (kinda) symphonic brass, it seems Alamaailman has made me a prog lover after all.
