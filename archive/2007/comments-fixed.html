Comments fixed

I'm stupid!!! I admit to it blatantly and frankly now. Remember the short post about how the stupid "subscribe to feedblitz" button hijacks the search function for apparently no reason (i haven't changed the code at all). It seems I forgot to remove it from the single post page when I removed it from the main page. So for the last few days I was wondering why there are no comments, because I was hoping to get a few comments on the design or how the radioshow is going but I was pretty sure at least yesterday's lovesongs compilation would get a few comments. But there was none. Just as I was blaming you the readers for being the most lazy bunch of guys I saw ever, I saw @ the logs that Submit Comments was also hijacked by feedblitz :(. Frankly I don't think it's got anything to do with feedblitz at all, but it's probably my own fault, but anyways it seems "Submit Comment" was the 35th most clicked link in the past month 25 days with 54 clicks.. Now please show me some wall to bang my head on, we've missed around 10 comments at least (provided that every commenter clicked 5 times to submit) and you know comments are a bloggers basic income. Anyways the comments are now working I've tested it twice and it seems to work. It won't bring past comments back, but hopefully it will bring future comments! So guys & gals I'm interested in how the radioshow is faring, is it too much freestyle, incoherent, not working? or are we good at this freestyle thing? and anything you want to tell about the site design, or things you tried to say in the past (desperate attempt to get past comments) please do so!
