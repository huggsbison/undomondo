August 17, 2007 · 2007

Vacation 2

Marc Ribot by Pennello Here's the second post I've backed up before I went on vacation, 2 great songs from 2 guitar giants. First Marc Ribot going wild with Yezriel, a bluesy song off his new album on Tzadik with a powerful riff on the wonderful Book of Angels series which previously saw the Jamie Saft Trio, Kobi Israelite and the Cracow Klezmer Orchestra among others. Assisted by Calvin Weston and Trevor Dunn on drums and bass respectively and playing songs composed by John Zorn for his second Masada Songbook. Next up is Mr. Scofield covering Rolling Stones' unforgettable single Satisfaction, on his Emarcy debut This Meets That which will be out in September.
