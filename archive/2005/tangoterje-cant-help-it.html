July 22, 2005 · 2005

Tangoterje - Can't help It

(from the "Can't help It" EP on G.A.M.M) Such a joy to have found this. I've been listening to this one for more than a month when i realized that I should get off my ass and share this definitive summer hit! (albeit in small circles) This is a cover (Hey, it's not a cheap commercial remix/bootleg to make money off an old hit, like most of the music industry does nowadays!)of Jacko's "Can't help it", and such a good one it is!. (produced by Stevie Wonder for him, cool!) Well MJ's singing was perfect in this era, the song was a hit, and the 70s disco strings and latin percussions added make this song shine. BTW Tangoterje is Todd Terje. and you would really regret it, if you pass this one. :) Download Tangoterje - I can't help it Buy the 12" here And also, i fucked up the Tiburon mp3 and posted an incomplete one. It's OK now, so you can safely download it below..
