October 3, 2005 · 2005

free yourself from music within a day...

..with the help of, who else, but mr. MichL Bridge, whom I've mentioned a few posts ago regarding his release on stroem. His last effort is a drony ambient piece on Luumu, which rotates around the concept of transformation. In each song he's taken one sample, and transformed it in time slowly. The music is very minimal, and it varies as if it's a natural transformation through time. That theme of slow change is IMO best visible on the opening track, where he's taken a marimba like sample and done some rhythmic variations to create an ambience. MichL has also added an accompanying text to the full archive (like the old demo scene text files, if you can recall), sharing his motives and mindset before and after the EP. I guess it's best to quote from his own text as a closing sentence; "i wanted the tracks to sound very simple and pure. they all should happen this special weekend to really proof that a kind of transformation is possible within that period of time. and to catch this feeling as true as possible. "
