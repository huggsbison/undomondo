November 27, 2005 · 2005

Sunday Roundup 2

Some nice sites for you to check out, kids.Many interesting things going on in the wonderful world of the net, especially the idea of universal findability, and RFID, object tagging, spime and the internet of things. Wow, what was once not even labelled as SF is now theoretically reality. I bet you have heard about it before I did, but if not there's a huge amount of related articles here. A witty social and collaborative art project. Insert object and out comes an artful replica. What's your project? is an online repository of creative DIY projects, continuing where the 52 Projects book left off. Docmaker on the Go is a documentary vlog, which features interviews with graffiti and hiphop artists mostly in South Bronx. It really is so easy to publish great video content now. Hurray! and now for some accompanying music. Every town and and village should have an Afrobeat Orchestra. Great modern afrobeat roundup on Soundroots. Dangerdoom coverage on Veritas Lux Mae. Nightmares on Wax - Les Nuits on PolloxNiner. It's an old song but never stale, if you belong to a generation who missed it, you should not miss it this time. Great netlabel coverup from Disconize Me!. Maxx Cavalera on Broque, Hachi on Interdisco, and Muhr on Serein. and videos on Buscate un Novio. Cocorosie Live in Hamburg and Mu. Great!
