December 8, 2005 · 2005

Netlabel releases - November Final

Last release I want to mention is by Gate Zero on the Stadtgruen netlabel. It's inspiration is some theoretical discussion what "rooms" are. I like theoretical & conceptual issues more than daily affairs for sure.. However I do get bored with theoretical talks in relation to music. After all the talk on the label page, I didn't find anything related to rooms more than other albums that don't talk about rooms. Point is; this is good old deep minimal electronica/techno, with a delicate usage of reverb and ambience and the feeling of space and noise around you. I really liked some tunes on this EP, and I'll make sure I get more releases by Gate Zero, but rooms? Well I still don't know much about them!.
