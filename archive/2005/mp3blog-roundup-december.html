mp3blog roundup - december

some useful links from the last 2 weeks :
  • Mothers against Noise is a site against noise. It's a funny site with the well known lame conservative rhetoric,"Noise is also a very dangerous musical trend that is hell bent on destroying civilized culture, this anti-cultural movement is quickly sweeping the globe, and is very dangerous to our youth." Please tell me this is a joke!
  • An old post from Adoru. I was thinking of posting Quasimoto's (aka Madlib) Greenery. A hilarious song on weed. However I find it here still existing on a post from 2004 here. Get it and then enjoy some more hiphop from Fatlip and Nas.
  • Lots of MP3s on Bavarian Open Source, including Cobra Killer, Dakar & Grinser and Bus feat MC Soom-T.
  • Tons of quality stuff from Mp3blogs.ch. Jason Forrest, RATN (Riow Arai and Tujiko Noriko), Wisp, Jasper TX, Desormais and more modern electronica, downtempo, IDM. Go Switzerland!
  • Party pictures! Yes we all love party pictures!. and we love it more, if it's from Europe. (Who doesn't love classy girls who don't shake their ass all the time, like there's no tomorrow?)
  • Ok, this one is in French, but with a very clean design that will make you feel you know French. Stuff like Headman, Tiga, Noia etc.
  • Get the latest last beat @ dirtyradio. via the official #thelastbeat.com blog
  • And last but not least, 3 free holiday tunes from the great Ghostly International label. Outputmessage - Carol of the Bells, Cepia - Christmas time is here
