May 16, 2005 · 2005

plantlife - when she smiles she lights the sky

(taken from their luv 4 the world EP) it seems i'm a bit late to jump on Plantlife, i just heard this EP, and i realized that they've won a best album award from Gilles Peterson last year, and countless comments from people like Pharell, Def Mos, Xzibit, and no it's not a hiphop album. It's some unique mixture of a soulful downbeat jazz disco hiphop hybrid, kind of stuff that you'd like to hear from Master Gilles. The LA band has already begun touring with Gilles Peterson and been covered everywhere intensively. This EP has some King Britt remixes, but i preferred to post the original. EXTRA : download plantlife - appreciate on their own record label's page, and then go check out the comments @ the official site here or check out Worldwide 2004 best album/track winners here
