July 3, 2005 · 2005

The words of an oak!

well after all the live 8 shows, you're probably having a music overload, and wanna rest your head without any music.. Think again!! you can't live without music, you know that.. Well once you come to terms with that, here's mix.no#4 from our friend hecanjog. Let me get it straight, it's been a long time since I stopped thinking djing is cool, but of course once in a while stuff like this happens to change my mind. These mixes span across many genres, from accoustic stuff to short passages of speech or noise, to electronic stuff, to avantgarde jazz to neo-folk and indie, it's almost like changing channels on the radio, albeit the random factor! and he also has an exquisite music taste of hard to come-by tracks most of the stuff that i'm sure you'd fall in love with at least 2 every mix, (at least!) Stuff from people like C.Penrose, Jandek, Kemialliset Ystävät, Murcof, Lau Nau, Lightning Bolt, Akira Rabelais and more, I hope I managed to get your attention by now so just go to this page and get all the 4 mixes while it lasts!
