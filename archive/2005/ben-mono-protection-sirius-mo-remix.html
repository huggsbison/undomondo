November 23, 2005 · 2005

Ben Mono - Protection (Sirius Mo Remix)

Great tune off a great compilation! French Distributor Wagram's International Music News compilation, is curated from labels such as !K7, Irma, Compost, Gomma, Raw Fusion etc.. It's a striking compilation, one of the best I've listened to in 2005. It's style ranges from house funk fusions such as this one to soulful downtempo of Alice Russel to Electro-house of Siskid to Hiphop from Dobie and more. This is an album that has no significant lo-points and covers a very wide range urban electronic music. The tunes are selected with a great taste and I seriously advice you to get yourself a copy if you want a true panaroma of "hip" European music. Download Ben Mono - Protection (Sirius Mo Remix)
