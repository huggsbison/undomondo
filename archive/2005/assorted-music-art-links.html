Assorted music // art links

A few quick notes :Monteaudio is a netlabel from Uruguay. The reference to Montevideo may be a little bit obvious there, but who else than the first netlabel of Uruguay deserves this name. They've got 2 releases on their site, Floor by Amdet and Ubicuidad by Cooptrol, lo-fi minimal electronica south americana! Video to Jamie Lidell's Multiply on Cliptip. Nothing spectacular but it's always nice to see the white funk soul brother being casual. Watch the video and then enjoy his official site, and also watch this video if you haven't as well, improvising with his vocals on stage, wooo.. Download Erik Satie : Conceptual Works on the Ubu Web archive This is the funniest cover I've ever listened to in my life, Jazz singer Nina Gordon singing NWA's Straight outta Compton, with accoustic guitar, including the curse words! Libelous claims about large corporations is about making libelous claims about large corporations, like Adidas uses cadaver feet to test new shoes or FedEx licks every package it handles. An interactive and immersive gaming / education system that's hopefully gonna be widespread in a few years. Takes place in an abandoned bank in Madrid, where the players are given RFID codes, and puzzles and problems to tackle while trying to escape from jail. cool! Still more to reblog tomorrow, ciao.
