deerhoof the great!

deerhoof, is teh funniest!! > i've read so many interviews and i've never read a serious answer, take a look @ this interview from pitchfork ;

"Do you want to say anything about Milk Man's song writing process? Things you did differently than in Reveille, and were there any specific reasons or conscious decisions to avoid things?"

Deerhoof: The process used to be that I would brush my teeth, go to bed, and in that world of twilight consciousness, start hearing melodies and things. On Milk Man the process was different, because I realized that it's also important to floss.

see?
