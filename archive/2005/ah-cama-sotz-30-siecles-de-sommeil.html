October 26, 2005 · 2005

Ah Cama-Sotz - 30 Siecles de Sommeil

Normally, I'm not too much into dark ambient - industrial stuff as you might guess from the lack of mentions on this blog. However this song is an exception. Ah Cama-Sotz, one of the bigger names in the industrial scene has released a new album recently, and this is the opening track. Quite haunting and disturbing as you would except, this track is devoid of any rhythm. Occasional strings and a dominant eastern vibe took me to the slave markets of the past, and what better way to complete it with a strange old vocal sample from a possible Turkish folk song. Suitable for the soundtrack of an evil Arabian nights, with djinns and efreets and all that!.
