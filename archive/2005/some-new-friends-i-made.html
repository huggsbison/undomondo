October 22, 2005 · 2005

Some new friends I made..

A few nice mp3blogs for your enjoyment.. * Schrikdraad from NL , from where you can find the links to download varied stuff like Ratatat, Bonobo, a Pink Floyd live from 1969, an Autechre bootleg, Neutral Milk Hotel, and Ini Kamoze?!? * The excellent xxxSurgeryxxx blog, which adds detailed electronic reviews and 50 minute mixes once in a while, featuring an excellent choice of tracks of minimal electronica, he's got 15 out, which includes tracks from Schwabinggrad Ballett, Akiyama/Kahn, Tujiko Noriko, Pan American and Porn Sword Tobacco.. What's more he archives his past shows, so don't you waste time because you never know when bandwidth's get exhausted. * Comfort Music is the blog of Comfort Radio featuring decent electronica, clash and trip-hop.. great relatively unknown finds (Luminescent Orchestrii, Finn. Chin Chin balanced with major players like Ladytron, Broken Social Scene and Goldfrapp) * And last but not least Aurgasm featuring the lovely Tin Hat Trio. If you like Mediterranean sounds with tango and accordeon, you're in for a treat.
