October 28, 2005 · 2005

Indie / Instrumental Rock Special #1 - God is an Astronaut

With the lack of any nice electronica to post, and general return to more guitar music for me for the last week, I think I would post some nice rock music.. I was going to mention Trail of Dead's Worlds Apart which is a great song, but Think Tank has got it covered, I thought about posting Animal Collective's Grass but that's covered by The Camera as Pen as well.. Some Saxon Shore? Well more than 3 songs have been posted by mp3blogs, so a 4th one would be too much (respect the artists!).. That's why I turn back to the beginning of the year, to a great album gone unmentioned by mp3blogs . That's God is an Astronaut's All Is Violent, All Is Bright album.. These 3 blokes from Dublin, (Mogwai anyone?) rocked my ears for so long, it would be a shame if I passed this song. (God it rhymes.. ) So here's a great atmospheric instrumental track for you, very melodic and melancholic.. I've got a few more tracks lined up here and I'm digging the crates for more, so stay tuned! Download God is an Astronaut - Fire Flies and Empty Skies
