June 21, 2005 · 2005

some recent net finds

time for some recent netsurf finds, i wonder how our lives would have been without the www. .
  • Konono No.1 video (thx to AudioMastermind)>> I'm 100% sure that you've got to watch this video!!!!. Konono No.1 is from Kinshasa/Congo and they do some kind of electric afro music, this video shows them playing music on the streets of Kinshasa, This is surely my best find from mp3blogs since i began reading them, thanks to fluxblog , you can still get this incredible afroelectronic song which got me started on Konono, and sorry to announce you it took us 25 years to hear about them.
  • Hofuku Sochi live video >> downtempo, lo-fi, electronica
  • Smart-music.net presents the Agriculture >> free netlabel compilation, including tracks by Sub Dub / DJ Rupture, dubby electronica.
  • This months Afrobeat/House/Nujazz mix from Freaked
  • 3 50hertz tracks on microlips mp3blog, 50 hertz is so elite! i.. must.. post..
  • The Bush-League Sessions on the Smashed Robot >> (artists include mylo, ragazzi, simian mobile disco, out hud, new order, lcd soundsystem)
  • Bloc Party in concert on NPR.
  • 2 Akron/Family tracks on the Borrowed Tunes mp3blog >> This has got to be one of the best releases since January, this 4 piece american folk band amazed me with their song writing ability, i hope to post one more track from them soon as well. .
enjoy all, and kudos to all the mp3bloggers around the world ;)
