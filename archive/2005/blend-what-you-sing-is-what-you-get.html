Blend - What you sing is what you get

Tags: athens , downtempo , jazz , funk , seventies
Sometimes you need a break from this problem ridden world.. A voice to assure you that things are getting better and you can now rest for a few minutes. This easy going song chanting "everything's gonna be alright" is just that!. When you listen to this song you'll be assured that it is going to be alright (provided it's a sunny day!) and you have an offbeat organ, a guitar, and some percussion.. Blend is the brainchild of George Mandas from Athens and is a sixpiece. if you like multicoloured and multiflavoured jazzfunky downtempo sounds, their new album is out on Sirius M41. Download Blend - What you sing is what you get Go to Blend Music Check out the netlabel Death to Spies which features Blend tracks among with other downtempo niceties from Papadog, Chix and the MLF.
