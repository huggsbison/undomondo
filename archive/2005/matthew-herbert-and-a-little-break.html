July 28, 2005 · 2005

Matthew Herbert and a little break

(from his Plat du Jour album on his own Accidental records) Well, the boss is back for a new album. You know you can never go wrong with Mr. Herbert, he is constantly experimenting, yet always sounding accessible and musical. This song is off his new album, it features Dani on vocals, and it's a satire about modern day celebrities (Go David! Victoria! Go Beyonce Go Beyonce!). Cheerful downtempo electronica with little bleeps and melodies going in the background, and with beautiful vocalwork from Dani.. And with that we conclude the intro to undomondo :) , I'll be away on vacation till the 2nd week of August, so no new posts till we make the greatest comeback known in the universe!
