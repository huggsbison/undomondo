September 29, 2005 · 2005

Netlabel releases for a rainy day

Hey, today I woke up with furious raining outside, and what better way to begin it with Tonepoem's Abandoned Amusements release on Earstroke netlabel. Dreamy, naive and mostly full of emotional melodies accompanying childhood memoirs, If you liked the Minotaur Shock album this year; you'd probably love to visit Mr. Rodinelli's imaginary landscape. Download Tonepoem - Abandoned Amusements Another suitable music for a rainy day should be, Miasmah 58 by Muhr: La chute du romantisme en ballades. Melancholic folk music, with delayed ambient guitar and string work reminiscent of many post-rock artists. It's romantic and depressing with a cinematic feeling. Soundtrack for a possible 15 minute short movie. Standout track for me is Le ciel sur les épaules, which is an ambient piece with strings and piano. Download Muhr - La chute du romantisme en ballades
