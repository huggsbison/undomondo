Netlabel releases - November 05

I know I've been lacking in this department for a while but today I checked my to read material from the netlabel releases group.Here's what I've discovered : Zvezdara's The Dawn of New Possibilities on Standard Klik Music. Very minimal almost ambient at times, this is suitable for reading or relaxing and what not. Deep and melancholic, with slow movement and shifts, I really liked their debut album. Congrats!
Download Le Temps N'Est Pas Importante Ici (mp3) Download the whole album as zip
A collaboration between Kyoto Sound and Instabil. This dubby minimal tune by Hieronymus just grabbed me and I made my mind to post about this EP, It's very refined and minimal, but if it's not your cup of tea, the remaining 3 tracks are great as well. 2 tracks by Daniel Stefanik and one by Xoki. If you're into dub without the dub-culture.. You will like this EP.
Download Friction Dub - Redirection on Instabil.
