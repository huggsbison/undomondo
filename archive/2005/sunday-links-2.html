Sunday Links 2

Just a few links before I go out to enjoy the biennial on this sunny day.
  • Interesting game concept called Grow!. No instructions, no visible goal. The order in you choose things, affect the outcome. There are many variations including a RPG one.
  • I'm pretty sure that if you ever owned that beautiful machine called Amiga, you're still reminiscing the old times. Here's a better chance, an archive of Amiga games (and not crap ones) in ADF format, which works with Winuae (or Winuaex). You can put them on your xbox and play, even better than having a working amiga with a disk drive!
  • Marvin Minsky, the co-founder of the MIT AI Lab, and one of the most prominent Artificial Intelligence visionaries, talking on his latest book "The emotion machine"
  • Online file sharers buy more music. From Guardian
  • A site full of live videos from bands such as Deerhoof and Lightning Bolt. (thx to Buscate un Novio)
  • I think I have failed to mention the Copy,Right? mp3blog before. It's an mp3blog covering, cover songs! Good rare finds here.
  • Heliodisplay is an interactive free-space multimedia projector. Well, just check out the videos if you think monitors and keyboards should be a thing of the past.
