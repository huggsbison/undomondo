What to do today!

* Read about our fave songwriter Mr. Sam 'Iron and Wine' Beam here and here. * Read a review of the illest girl in town. Kevin Blechdom's latest album 'Eat my heart out'. * And while you're at it, why not read reviews of CocoRosie's and Devandra's latest albums on Stylus Mag . Both worth the hype! * Get out of the house! * What's this ?! An audio-visual experimental project by Golan Levin and Zachary Lieberman. Many more here.. * Another impressive project by the duo above. Or maybe impressive is not the right word? Unbelievable, might be better. * Subscribe to the Mondo Globo podcast, for commentary on copyright, politics and philosophy. * Last but not least, read an interview with the prolific social thinker Hakim Bey. "So they're perfectly happy to see the old farms close down and build McMansions, as long as they're green McMansions, of course, with maybe a little solar power so they can boast about how they are almost off the grid. This is just yuppie poseurism. It's fashionable to be green, but it's not at all fashionable to wonder about the actual working class and farming people and families that you're dispossessing. This is a class war situation, and the artists are unfortunately not on the right side of the battle. If we would just honestly look at what function we're serving in this economy, I'm afraid we would see that we're basically shills for real estate developers."
