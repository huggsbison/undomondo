November 3, 2005 · 2005

Kevin Blechdom - Invisible Rock

Stop press, a little break between the recent rock/indie features provided by who other than our favourite lass Kevy B. This is a silly electro-pop song, very raw synth brasses and downward weird-o (and funny) lyrics got me hooked, (I got a secret love // it's my invisible rock) cheerful, but I'm pretty sure some of you will find it very kitsch and not like it.. oh well you can't please everyone.. ! And what's the deal with the tit exposure at every concert? Is she morphing from the smart inaccessible IDM grrl personality to a shock rock artist? is there anything as an IDM grrl? you decide..
