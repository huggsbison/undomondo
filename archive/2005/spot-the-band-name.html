Spot the band name

I don't know if you've seen this, but as an mp3blogger, I think it's my duty to at least mention this marvellous ad from Virgin commemorating their anniversary. This ad contains 72 band names, literally, on this majestic illustration. It takes a while to think in the same the way the creators did, taking the band names as literary as possible, but once you did that, it's laughter galore..Thanks for whoever created this, great idea and great realization, and thanks to Dreadfuldan from flickr, for blogging this, his page has the full answers as well.
