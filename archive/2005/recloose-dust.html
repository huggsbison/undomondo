June 13, 2005 · 2005

Recloose - Dust

(from his Dust 12" on Peacefrog) After a few days of rest without a computer, here are some new selections that has cheered me up during that time. This is an incredible toon (note my devious mixture of tune and cartoon) with a jazzy organ and horn stabs, a groovy bassline and witty lyrics, it's almost impossible to resist the cartoonish mood of this song, don't be mistaken this is not jazz but a very eclectic mixture, it's a perfect dancy beginner for a sunny day and i'm not sure if you can resist the urge to repeat this song for at least 3 times in a row. Alright my words don't do it enough justice i know, but this goes straight to the "top 2005 songs" bin. This 12" includes a 7 minute main mix, which is the real deal. The vocals are by Joe Dukie from Fat Freddy's Drop which is a 7 piece dub band from New Zealand. EXTRA : you can listen to a live recording by Recloose @ milkaudio (Real Media)
