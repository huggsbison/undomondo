Mabafu - Bahia Aleman

Is Mabafu's latest online offering Bahia Aleman referencing Senor Coconut's El Baile Aleman (Mr. Schmidt's Brasil covers of Kraftwerk songs, which is a masterpiece in itself.) Yes probably and not only limited to its title, because this is one of the best netlabel releases I've listened to in December (although it wasn't released in December i guess) Mabafu created easy listening downtempo songs for us fusioning various brasil styles and dub, and with a more than decent mastering (Thanks to Sofasound). This is a solid album, which sets the standard for future netlabel releases IMO.
