June 13, 2005 · 2005

Slope ft. Capital A - Komputa Groove

(12" single from their Komputa Groove LP on Sonar Kollektiv) Well if the Recloose track wasn't enough for you, here's another brilliant song, this time from Sonar Kollektiv's 2-piece Slope. Very innovative song, you would immediately feel these boys have established their own style. Cheerful mood again, in 2/4 shuffle beat with filtered keys, and pseudo-strings. If you are amazed by the first 1:30 of this song just wait till Capital A begins his rap-song hybrid. The flipside of the vinyl is also an incredibly eclectic boogie monster with Ovasoul 7's vocals. I'm dying to get my hands on the full LP release, which is as big as the Metro Area's self titled album release in 2002. You can listen to excerpts from all the songs on the LP here on Sonar Kollektiv's site. (respect!)
