July 16, 2005 · 2005

Ammon Contact - Naeem

(from their 2005 CD New Birth) well we all have learnt by now that not all music should be party music, or phenomenal in success to be labelled good. We love those simple, moody, cinematique music that goes seemingly unnoticed, but your brain memorizes every beat or melody as a background process.. ammoncontact's latest offering on the legendary ninja tune has 9 tracks (LP has 10) of this calibre, a subtle mix of downtempo (with subtle hints of 70s soul and funk) electronica and instrumental hiphop, from the Los Angeles duo. It's a head-bopper for sure!.. this song has an incredible guitar lick (that gets stuck on your head instantly!) going on a nice bassline , and it will make you happy for 2.19 minutes :) Download Ammon Contact - Naeem Read a review of Ammon Contact - New Birth.
