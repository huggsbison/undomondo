Vacation re:blog

As a second post, just wanted to share with you some stuff, i've found on mp3blogs after i came back, so it's kind of a reblog of the last 10 days or so. Enjoy. PS : none of the links are direct links to mp3s, they're links to the posts! * Remote Viewer - 3 songs including "They're closing down" on Just for a day (folktronica) * Sunday Morning #8 compilation on Buscate on Novio including tracks by bikinikill, cocorosie, stereolab * Evil Robot Samba video on Tuning (an evil robot growling Girl from Ipanema!!!!!!!!!Fun!!!!) * Cool Analog Sequencer Table on our favorite blog Music Thing (new addition to the analog interface madness with max/msp.. the future looks great) * Popolous on Aurgasm (a very pretty find, i thought about posting tracks from Popolous before, but postponed it instead, congrats to aurgasm!) (hiphop, electronica) * Ralph Myerz and The Jack Herren Band - L.I.P.S.T.I.C.K on Fluxblog (bossa, disco) * Boozoo Bajou on Aurgasm (downtempo, bossa) * Matthias Sweitzer - Pluies on CredCentral (electronica with no beats and melodies galore) and more tracks on the official site * Laplaceausoleil - "White Rabbit" on Synergy Networks Netlabel (via Disconize Me) * still drooling on these speakers (via audiomastermind) * incredibly funny bootleg of John Lennon's Imagine sung by George W.Bush on audiomastermind and to wrap it all up, posts from a wonderful album that was released last year. Tender deep house & nu-jazz with delicious vocals by Yukimi Nagano of Koop. * Hird - Keep you Kimi (and 2 more) posted by The Sean Show.
