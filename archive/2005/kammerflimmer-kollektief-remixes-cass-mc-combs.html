Kammerflimmer Kollektief Remixes // Cass Mc Combs

Todays update features two different vibes. NÄ´ze's remix is from a new release on Staubgold. Die Kollektief is remixed on this EP by Jan Jelinek, David Matt and Aoki Takamasa. Noze's remix begins as a mellow 4/4 track with spectacular brasswork from the original on top of minimal beats and suddenly changes into a dancefloor tune after 2:30 after the bass and vocals enter. Get Kammerflimmer Kollektief - Lichterloh remix by NÄ´ze (mp3) Available on Staubgold Second track is by Cass Mc Combs, I had heard of the name in Vice at the beginning of the year but it took me awhile to get a hold of it. This is an uptempo surfrock meets indie w/distorted guitar song. I don't know what that means, but the song is spectacular.
