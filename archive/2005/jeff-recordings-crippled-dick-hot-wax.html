August 16, 2005 · 2005

Jeff Recordings - Crippled Dick Hot Wax

Today i have two special world music songs from a rare and unique compilation on the CDHW label. Both of these songs have been engineered by Gerhard "Jeff" Nieckau, a German sound-engineer, who, in order to explore new music during the 70s set out on a journey to Peru and Trinidad & Tobago, and is also known to be the engineer behind Bossa 70 from Peru. What a wonderful idea for a compilation!. Ranging from calypso to afrobeat and more, the tracks sound raw and they still have the marks of history on them. Should you like these two tracks below, i'm 100% positive you'd be delighted to have this album permanently for your archive. :) And you should definitely read about Mr. Jeff Nieckau (born 1923), one of the pioneers of sound engineering and open-mindedness (something we still need in music biz!)
