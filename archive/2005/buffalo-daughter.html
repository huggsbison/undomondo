Buffalo Daughter

hi kids! i am error101 and this is my first post so be kind:)I wanna tell you about Buffalo Daughter now. Although I just found them out, it seems that they're a Japanese band from Tokyo that's been around since '94. (for those of you who don't remember, that's before being a jap-band was hip.) Anyways, their most recent album "I" is from 2001 (on Emperor Norton) but it sounds as it's from much before; as if it's a futuristic album from the 90s. New ideas done with old technology. Certainly not over-produced, they have a raw sound with basic synths, robotic voices and many more classics that'll probably never be outfashioned in our modern world of music. Oh also they don't really have a strict style since it ranges from pop-punk, jap-rock and electro-disco. well, 'nuff said, i'll leave you with two songs from "I" now. I shortened the name of "robot sings". the long name is "robot sings (as if he were Frank Sinatra with a harf-baild egg and the salt shaker on a breakfast table)". have a nice day:)
