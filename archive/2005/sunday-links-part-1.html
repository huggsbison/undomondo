Sunday Links - Part 1

I can't stop thinking #6 - Thought provoking web-comic on pirating, copyright and music industry.. We're going into a new era and we should really start thinking about some alternative economical models. Sofa as MIDI Controller - If two people are sitting down, they can output three control values each - left and right buttock weight and how far the person is leaning back. DIY Spirit rocks on! (via Music Thing) Balldroppings - A generative audio-visual game, where balls on your screen makes rhythmic sounds, it's like playing a game with reaktor :) nice design. Pitchfork review of Isolee's We are Monster
The CD Sequencer by Matthew Falla - WOW, what a novel idea. The CD Sequencer is an innovative new concept for interactive CD packaging. You can use printed postcards to remix music and store your mixes or share them with friends. Draw on the cards to remix the tunes. If you are happy with a mix, you can remove the card and save it for later. The remix is "stored" on the card until the next time you insert it, when it immediately plays back. Kracfive - Iron Chef of Music battles - electronic music battles from Kracfive artists, all songs are available as mp3s.. artists include Kettel, Colongib, Khonnor and more.. also check out their monthly mp3 releases here
