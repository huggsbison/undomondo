December 14, 2005 · 2005

Jahcoozi - Ali Mc Bills

Alright, I've been beaten by 2 other mp3blogs for Jahcoozi, Largehearted Boy has got the track I wanted to post and BigStereo has got a more detailed write up than mine, but there are so many gems in this recent Kitty Yo release, no one can stop me from posting another track! (well technically). Jahcoozi is a multinational trio from Berlin. Their fusion of downtempo, hiphop, dub and electro is aspired by many groups around the world. However, while most fail to deliver the goods, Jahcoozi has managed to create a CD full of non-skippable tracks. Most of the tracks here are interesting, and the remaining few are pleasant to say the least. Listen to these guys, an unexpected competitor for my top 20 2005 albums. The featured track is Alli Mc Bills, an hypnotizing dub track with great lyrics, I'm sure you'll like it..
