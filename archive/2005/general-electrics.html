September 11, 2005 · 2005

General Electrics

Great EP on Compost by the French keyboardist Hervé Salters aka General Electrics. 4 original songs and 2 remixes on this one. The Compost website labels his music as Sunday Morning goodness and it really feels good to listen to these tracks on a sunday morning like this. The song Catch the Bus begins with a noisy synth and then mutates into a 70s spygroove , lounge track. Terms and Conditions remix by The Entity on the other end is a top-notch track. An excellent shuffle-beat recreates the song in German territory. Not the usual compost style, but if you like the AtomTM works on Rather Interesting or if you went to Sonar this year, you should like these tracks. :) Download Catch the Bus Download Terms and Conditions (The Entity Remix)
