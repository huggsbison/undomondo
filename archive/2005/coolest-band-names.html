Coolest band names!

I really think there's a correlation between the coolness of a band name and the coolness of the music they do.. I've been intrigued many times by the name of a band or person, and tried to find out if they sound as good as their name.. And most of the time, i've not been disappointed. Yesterday I saw a band named "Stop coming to my house Prom Queen" and I thought "Hey why don't I make a post about weird&cool band names", and ended up doing this. So feel free to comment if you wanna add some weird named and cool band/groups, in fact PLEASE do so. :) Here is the first 10 randomly ordered..
  1. stop coming to my house prom queen - (apparently it looks like 2 mogwai song names put together :) )
  2. weight and measures is an instrumental rock band from ottawa
  3. wevie stonder is a funny electronica band on SKAM/Schematic
  4. cream abdul babar is an 8 piece hardcore/metal band (with a trombone player!)
  5. a. silver mt. zion - you surely know about this postrock band, formed by some members of a yet another weird named post rock band godspeed you black emperor.
  6. brothers of the occult sisterhood - the occult sisterhood?
  7. my americas watching tigers die is a great newschool hardcore band, but what tigers???
  8. deerhoof - deerhoof my love!
  9. get cape, wear cape, fly - this one is an instant fav. I wonder if that line is from an original text adventure.. laptop based melodic electronica with vocals.
  10. 7 year rabbit cycle - noise rock from ex-deerhoof members ..
