About

Undomondo started as a music blog & radioshow in 2005, to promote independent music regardless of genres, periods and geography. It evolved into a radioshow and then became my selector name.

Undomondo music diet covers quite a lot of dishes from jazz, folk, world music to exotica, afro-latin, brasil, easy listening and beyond. While DJing it's more dance oriented spanning all kinds of weird electronic stuff to old school house and even techno.
