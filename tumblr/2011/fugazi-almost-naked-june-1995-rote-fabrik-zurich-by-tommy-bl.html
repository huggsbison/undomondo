Fugazi almost naked! (June 1995, “Rote Fabrik”, Zürich) (by Tommy Blank)

The show was over, but the audience was shouting so loud that Fugazi had to be convinced by their support-band A.S.E. to come back on stage from the shower! While they were playing, Hno stole whatever else was left from their catering. The following week we made good money on tour selling “apples from the hands of Holy Ian.”
