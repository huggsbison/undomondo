Africa Hitech - Out In The Streets

Africa Hitech - Out In The Streets

Mark Pritchard (aka Harmonic 313 and one half of Global Communication alongside Tom Middleton)and Steve Spacek’s Africa Hitech released their debut ‘93 Million Miles’ on Warp last month to critical acclaim. Raw Detroit techno meets, post-dubstep via Africa and Chicago Juke.
