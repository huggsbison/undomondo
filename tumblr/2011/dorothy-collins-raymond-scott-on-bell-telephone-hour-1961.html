Dorothy Collins, Raymond Scott on Bell Telephone Hour (1961)

Dorothy Collins, Raymond Scott on Bell Telephone Hour (1961) (by collinsfollower)

The Bell Telephone Hour televised a program entitled Designs in Music on December 8, 1961. Dorothy Collins and her husband Raymond Scott along with his quintette appear and perform songs like I Got Lost In His Arms,  Song of India, Singing in the Rain, Powerhouse and Then I’ll Be Happy.
