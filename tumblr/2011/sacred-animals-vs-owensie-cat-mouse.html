Sacred Animals vs Owensie - Cat & Mouse

Sacred Animals vs Owensie - Cat & Mouse
(by Feel Good Lost)

One off collaboration by two Irish acts, Dublin’s Sacred Animals and Michael Owens aka Owensie, involving a sad story about the death of a Russian refugee family. Video by Brendan Canty of Feel Good Lost.

“Cat And Mouse’ was written about a Russian family who fell to their death in a housing estate in Glasgow last year. An apparent suicide from high in an old tower block used to house asylum seekers, it is thought they may have jumped for fear of deportation by immigration authorities.”
