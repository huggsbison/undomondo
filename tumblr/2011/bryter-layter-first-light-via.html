Bryter Layter - “First Light” via slow-swords

“Well, it looks like Students of Decay is going to be putting out another slab of fantastic wax soon, if this is anything to go by. Bryter Layter is the duo of Joe Raglani and Mike Pollard, so it logically follows that this is going to be one kickass display of synth goodness. Gorgeous video done by John Davis, which fantastically layers color over black and white, to go alongside the hypnotic piece of calming tones and slow motion rhythms. Watch out for Two Lenses, coming to some sort of record store near you (at least somewhat near you, hopefully).”
