Best of 2011 (Part 1)

Best of 2011 (Part 1)
Psych/Kosmiche/Tropical/Experimental

Peaking Lights - 936 (Not Not Fun)
Lee Noble - Horrorism (Bathetic)
Borden, Ferraro, Godin, Halo & Lopatin - FRKWYS Vol. 7 (RVNG)
Stag Hare - Spirit Canoes (Hands in the Dark/ Inner Islands)
Javelin - Canyon Candy EP (Luaka Bop)
ADR - Solitary Pursuits (Public Information)
Rangers - Pan Am Stories (Not Not Fun)
Plankton Wat - In magical light (Reverb Worship)
Master Musicians of Bukkake - Totem Three (Important)
Mountains - Air Museum (Thirll Jockey)
Niggas With Guitars - Ethnic Frenzy (Digitalis)
Oneohtrix Point Never - Replica (Software)
Isidore Ducasse - Self Titled (Blackest Rainbow)
Seth Horvitz - Eight Studies for Automatic Piano (Line)
Golden Retriever - Light Cones (Thrill Jockey)

936 was destined to be on the end of year lists from the get go anyway, however 2011 was Lee Noble’s year. Horrorism was easily one of the, and his latest ‘Persona’ is mindblowing as well. Keep your eyes peeled for him!.
