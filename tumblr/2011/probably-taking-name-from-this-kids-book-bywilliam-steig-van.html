Probably taking name from this kids book byWilliam Steig, Vancouver’s Brave Irene is the brainchild

Probably taking name from this kids book by William Steig, Vancouver’s Brave Irene is the brainchild of indie underground Rose Melberg and four best friends. Some of the best twee / power pop I’ve listened to in ages with great songwriting albeit sounding a little childish? . Out on Slumberland, one of my favourites alongside HoZac for everything lo-fi & garagey.
