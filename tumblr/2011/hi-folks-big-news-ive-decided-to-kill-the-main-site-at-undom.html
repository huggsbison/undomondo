Hi folks, big news! I’ve decided to kill the main site at undomondo.com

The story begins like this. More than 15 days ago I got a ticket from the hosting company that my wordpress database is using too much resource and they disabled access to it.

After countless emails back and forth which created a loop of getting access only to see it disabled once again via an automated ticket, I decided to move everything to tumblr.  It took too much time to edit and after 6 years I didn’t have the energy anyway. However as is expected I couldn’t manage to install tumblr on the domain. I changed the A Record and have been waiting for days but today I can’t even see the domain on Godaddy.

So thank you hostgator and godaddy. I got what I deserved for using your services.

If everything goes right, I might restore wordpress and get the archive back, just so that I can still brag I was once a semi successful blogger while picking up girls. 

Thank you for listening. Now go fuckin’ listen to the new Thee Oh Sees.
