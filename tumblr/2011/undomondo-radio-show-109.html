Undomondo Radio Show #109

Undomondo Radio Show #109

#109 is more or less consisted of music I had on shuffle while in Barcelona for Primaverasound. A lot of sunshine music from ambient and electronic acts like Eachothers, James Blake and Gang Gang Dance and lo-fi garage bands like Davila 666, Thee Oh Sees, Sonny & The Sunsets, Girls Names, The People’s Temple and Black Lips. Enjoy!
