David Letellier aka Kangding Ray - VERSUS

David Letellier aka Kangding Ray - VERSUS
(by kangding ray)

Versus is a sound installation consisting of two kinetic sculptures placed face to face. Each sculpture is made out of 12 triangular panels, hinged and powered by six linear actuators, controlled by a specific program.

At regular intervals, each sculpture produces a sound, simultaneously recorded and analyzed by the opposite, which then moves according to the frequencies of this sound. Like a feedback loop, it then plays back the recorded sound, with the errors and disturbances caused by the reverberating space and the visitors.
