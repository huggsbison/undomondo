St. Etienne - Only Love Can Break Your Heart  (by SaintEtienneOfficial) (via 01blog)

In 1990, English band Saint Etienne recorded a cove version of Neil Young’s song “Only Love Can Break Your Heart”, which would be included on their debut album Foxbase Alpha. Their recording, made in under two hours, got them a record deal, their first single, and their first hit.
