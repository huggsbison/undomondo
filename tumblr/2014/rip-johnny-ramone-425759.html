RIP Johnny Ramone

zombiesenelghetto:

zombiesenelghetto:

People have asked me, “What makes a punk?” After we’d retired, I was driving in Los Angeles, and somebody called out to me, “Hey, you’re driving a Cadillac. How’s that? How are you a punk if you’re driving a Cadillac?” I said, “What the fuck are you talking about? I wrote the book on punk. I decide what’s punk. If I’m driving a Cadillac, it’s punk.”

RIP Johnny Ramone (October 8, 1948 – September 15, 2004), photo by Jim Leddy, March 1976
