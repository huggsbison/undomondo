Unknown User: Hi there, I recently put out a math rock/post-rock experimental album called "Brand New Skinny Ghost, Meet Your Brand New Angel Monster" under the name "Captain of the Swedish Team". It was written, performed, and recorded by me over a two week period in my basement apartment in Toronto Canada. It's mostly instrumental, with spoken word bits in between songs taken from a rant of my roommate I recorded. I have no plans to tour any time in the near future, so any online promotion I can get is much appreciated. Feel free to contact me if you have any questions, comments, what have you. You can listen to the album at http://captainoftheswedishteam.bandcamp.com Thanks a lot! -Aaron Corbett http://www.desertislandfiverecords.com
