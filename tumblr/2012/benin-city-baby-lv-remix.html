Benin City - Baby (LV Remix)

Benin City - Baby (LV Remix)

Upcoming release on Audio Doughnuts, a new collaboration from Joshua Idehen aka Benin City and LV. 

“Benin City is Joshua Idehen’s day job, a musical project combining spoken word with delicately wrought electronica. Benin City’s original version of ‘Baby’ was a bruised bit of urban yearning straight from the Ghostpoet school of broken hearts. LV’s remix is markedly perkier.”  FACT
