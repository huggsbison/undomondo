Ian Nagoski - FatCat Records Podcast

Ian Nagoski’s 78RPM mix:

“Over the past six years, I’ve released four CDs and six LPs of music from 78rpm discs.  The music here is all from about 1910-1950 and originates in Georgia (in the US), Greece, Poland, Quebec, Switzerland, India, Macedonia, Armenia, Korea, Germany, Turkey, Scotland, Zimbabwe, Italy, Syria, and Mexico." Mixcloud.
