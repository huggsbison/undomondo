“Moholy-Nagy is the moniker of San Francisco/Berlin-based musicians Jefre Cantu-Ledesma (The Alps), Danny Paul Grody (The Drift) and Trevor Montgomery (Lazarus, The Drift). The trio are the founding members of Tarentel—a group that helped inspired a decade’s worth of widescreen ambient rock—and Like Mirage reunites them for the first time since that group’s sophomore album, The Order of Things, was released almost a decade ago. Recorded and mixed by Phil Manley of Trans Am and The Fucking Champs, Like Mirage bottles a vibrant landscape that recalls the late 70’s explosion of Cosmiche, New Age, and Krautrock music as if it were a sunset descending on the damp streets of San Francisco.” Temporary Residence
