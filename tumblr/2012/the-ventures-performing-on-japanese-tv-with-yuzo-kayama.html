The Ventures performing on Japanese TV with Yuzo Kayama

The Ventures performing on Japanese TV with Yuzo Kayama (by weedsan21)

Surf had developed into a full fledged mania in mid-‘60s Japan, resulting in hundreds of Japanese combos during the era now dubbed ’Eleki Buumu’. Here we have a TV footage from '94, three decades after the boom, which kicks off with the iconic Japanese surf figure Yūzō Kayama.
