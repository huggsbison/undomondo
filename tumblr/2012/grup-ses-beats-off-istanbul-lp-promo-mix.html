Grup Ses Beats - Off Istanbul LP promo mix

Grup Ses Beats - Off Istanbul LP promo mix

So you think you know İstanbul. Off İstanbul is here to tell you may be you don’t. In a city with such transitions that make one feel he or she knows nothing at all, music works as a spring that collects, renders, digests and spews out influences in strong, independent and original ways. Here is Off İstanbul with 9 bands from this beautifully twisted city that feeds on all sides of the coin. From crackly relics of the past, to electrified ghosts from the future, the broken sounds these people create privately in their rooms or studios; reflect the city’s chaotic nature in a way that each of the band has its own vision of the mixed cultures they’ve been feeding on between past and future, old and new, Asia and Europe, boys and girls, laughter and tears etc. This is how Off İstanbul is made; not with a bang but a whimper.
