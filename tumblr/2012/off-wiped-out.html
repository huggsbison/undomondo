April 17, 2012 · 2012

OFF! - Wiped Out

OFF! - Wiped Out
(by noisey)

The idea of OFF! featuring ex-members of Circle Jerks, Blag Flag and Rocket from the Crypt sounds really cool on paper, and although the track simply has the energy, and Keith still rocks, I’m not really in tune with the idea that this could “bring back everything we loved about old school west coast skate culture”.

New album out in May, and they’ll be touring with the reformed Refused.
