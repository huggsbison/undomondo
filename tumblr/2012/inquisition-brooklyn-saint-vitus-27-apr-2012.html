Inquisition - Brooklyn, Saint Vitus 27 Apr 2012

Inquisition - Brooklyn, Saint Vitus 27 Apr 2012
(by (((unartig))))

The evilest black metal band I’ve seen this year is Colombia based duo Inquisition. Their ‘Ominous Doctrines of the Perpetual Mystical Macrocosm’ album has been selected the best bm album in 2010 on RYM. The vocals sound nastier here compared to the album and Dagon really scares my ass off. This video should be, Astral Path to Supreme Majesties, the opener to their 2010 album. Another video here.
