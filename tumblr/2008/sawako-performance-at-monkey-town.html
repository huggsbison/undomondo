Sawako performance at monkey town

https://www.youtube.com/embed/EeT5eAQLtcE?feature=oembed&enablejsapi=1&origin=http://safe.txmblr.com&wmode=opaque

Sawako performance at monkey town (via aliaspail)

Monkey town is a bar/restaurant with a room focused on presenting quality audio/visual shows. the sets are usually reservation only, maintaining a controllable audience, with entree order mandatory to watch the performance. snazzy dishes and a room boasting 4 screens enclosure, 6.1 surround sound, and limited capacity of 32 guests. the space serves as the perfect medium for sawako’s material. this time sawako was performing her own visual effects, i think using jitter. the visual’s were beautiful, moving from lights to negatives, feeling much like the music was passing through night and day.
