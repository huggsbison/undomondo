Embryo & Radio Afghanistan improvising in Kabul - 1978 (via ekp34)

A meeting between members of the group Embryo and members of Radio Afghanistan, the now legendary and long gone Afghan ensemble.
Christian Burchard-marimbafon, Roman Bunka-guitar, Ustad Mohammad Omar-rubab, Malang Negrabi-zerbaghali, and perhaps Ghazi-tabla. Performing an improvisation based on an Afghani tune. Music for all. Filmed on October 28 1978 in Kabul. Excerpt from the 1979 Werner Penzel film “Vagabunden Karawane”
