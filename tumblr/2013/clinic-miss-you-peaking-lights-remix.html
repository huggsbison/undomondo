Clinic - Miss You (Peaking Lights Remix)

Clinic - Miss You (Peaking Lights Remix)

Miss You is the first single taken from Clinic’s forthcoming seventh album, Free Reign out now. Creeping along obsessively like a heavily medicated Invaders, Miss You is an off kilter slow jam that evokes classic Clinic whilst taking the transportive craft of Ade Blackburn and co. to strange new frontiers. Here you can listen to Peaking Lights inner-space trance reworking.
