DJ Rashad - Rollin’

DJ Rashad - Rollin’

My first introduction to DJ Rashad, and I have only slightly brushed with footwork or juke music yet this totally blew my mind. The 4 track EP is a great treat if you wanna get acquainted.

“Of course this is footwork, of course it’s Teklife-affiliated. This is the Hyperdub show, after all, and we all know a DJ Rashad EP has been in the pipes for a while (you can find the track they’re playing on there). Now that you mention it, it couldn’t have been anyone else.”
