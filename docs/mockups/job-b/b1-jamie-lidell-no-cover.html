jamie lidell - what's the use

well, his new album is really the new sensation!.. I had always known about him, but categorized him in the inaccessible technoish music folder in my head, (a la cristian vogel, marcin czubala .. dunno why). Last year everybody was talking about his hyper-live show, including Isolee (he was blown away by his live show), and now his new album Multiply is out, and it sure is accessible. It's a soulful groovy laidback motown electronica hybrid. Reminded me a bit of Burnt Friedman & The Nu-dub Players. I'm surprised, i'm really surprised. The song "multiply" has been automatic repetition in my head for the last 2 weeks, but i'm gonna promo another one, (you can find "multiply" on fluxblog, if the links haven't died, yet, and you *should* it's a mega-hit in my opinion.) ok best of all, i found the link to his live show video (it wasn't that hard!) here at warp's site.. if you have the smallest idea to do a liveshow you should check this out, because the bar has been raised now! :) you can read a gig review here , and an interview with the man here
